**Title:** SymMeta: A Geometric Inner Loop for Adapting Frozen VLAs Across Robots

**Abstract (249 words):**
A frozen vision-language-action (VLA) policy can fail on a new arm with an unknown command-frame rotation, actuator gain, or gripper polarity. We consider proprioceptive motor babbling (ReGround, Siddique et al., 2026) and in-context behavior adaptation (Yang, Finn & Sadigh, 2026). We prove that local proprioception alone cannot identify the rotation. In our experiments, monolithic Perceiver encoders memorize discrete training shifts. Even with 18x more training embodiments (7 to 128), increasing capacity fourfold yields at most 20% out-of-distribution (OOD) recovery.

We introduce SymMeta, a symmetry-structured meta-adapter for frozen pi_0.5 and pi_0. A learned visual reader estimates motion from dense optical flow with SO(2)-invariant vertical parallax subtraction. Its geometric inner loop aligns that motion with issued commands by closed-form differentiable SO(2)/SO(3) Procrustes, while separate heads estimate gain and gripper polarity. Adaptation uses 0.75 seconds (15 controls) of passive motion with no target demonstrations or policy fine-tuning. Trained strictly on 7-DoF Panda+Sawyer, the exact same frozen checkpoints transfer zero-shot across 3 unseen robot arms (6-DoF UR5e, 7-DoF IIWA, 7-DoF Kinova3), 2 frozen VLAs (3-seed n=288 per benchmark: 78.1+/-2.3%, 78.5+/-1.0%, 66.3+/-2.7%, and 55.6+/-3.4% vs. 6.2-8.3% zero-shot shifted and 75-100% zero-shot unshifted), and 5 LIBERO-Pro2.0 visual/camera perturbations (S13-S19; 8,700+ live episodes), beating Horn-Schunck optical flow by +10.0 to +25.0 pp (all 95% CIs strictly positive), OpenCV DIS+Procrustes, Broyden UVS, Perceiver-ICBA (2.1-5.7% OOD across 3 seeds), ReGround (4.2-6.2%), and matching K=3 few-shot target-UR5e MLP SFT (78.1% same-shift) while avoiding SFT's 0.0% cross-shift collapse, while extending to full 3D SO(3) (+31.2 pp over Nominal) and ManiSkill3/SAPIEN.
