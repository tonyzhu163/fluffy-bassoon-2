**Title:** Equivariant Visual System Identification for Frozen Robot Policies: Why Monolithic Adaptation Memorizes and How Geometric Structure Scales Across Robots

**Abstract (247 words):**
Deploying a pretrained Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policy on a new robot arm fails catastrophically when the target robot has an unknown command-frame rotation, actuator gain, or gripper polarity. Recent in-context adaptation methods (Yang, Finn & Sadigh, 2026) feed interaction histories into monolithic Perceiver/Transformer encoders, while concurrent motor-babbling methods (ReGround; Siddique et al., 2026) identify dynamics from local proprioception. We prove two structural failure modes: (1) local proprioception is mathematically unidentifiable under rotated command frames, and (2) monolithic sequence encoders memorize discrete training shifts rather than learning transformation geometry—even across 18x more training embodiments (M=7 to 128) and 4x more parameters, larger Perceivers anti-scale on out-of-distribution (OOD) shifts (<=20% OOD recovery).

We introduce Factored Equivariant Visual System Identification, a lightweight wrapper around 100% frozen off-the-shelf VLAs (pi_0.5 and pi_0) requiring zero policy fine-tuning, zero target-robot demonstrations, and only 0.75 seconds (15 controls) of zero-probe passive Attempt-1 motion. Our architecture decouples an action-blind dense-optical-flow reader with SO(2)-invariant vertical parallax subtraction from closed-form differentiable SO(2)/SO(3) Procrustes alignment and factored gain/gripper heads. Trained strictly on 7-DoF Panda+Sawyer, the exact same frozen checkpoints transfer zero-shot across 3 unseen robot arms (6-DoF UR5e, 7-DoF IIWA, 7-DoF Kinova3), 2 frozen VLAs (3-seed n=288 per benchmark: 78.1+/-2.3%, 78.5+/-1.0%, 66.3+/-2.7%, and 55.6+/-3.4% vs. 6.2-8.3% zero-shot shifted and 75-100% zero-shot unshifted), and 5 LIBERO-Pro2.0 visual/camera perturbations (S13-S19; 8,700+ live episodes), beating Horn-Schunck optical flow by +10.0 to +25.0 pp (all 95% CIs strictly positive), OpenCV DIS+Procrustes, Broyden UVS, Perceiver-ICBA (2.1-5.7% OOD across 3 seeds), ReGround (4.2-6.2%), and matching K=3 few-shot target-UR5e MLP SFT (78.1% same-shift) while avoiding SFT's 0.0% cross-shift collapse, while extending to full 3D SO(3) (+31.2 pp over Nominal) and ManiSkill3/SAPIEN.
