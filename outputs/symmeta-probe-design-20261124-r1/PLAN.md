## Priors

With a rank-one first command, equal dose and an isotropic static response, adding perpendicular energy is a classical way to improve conditioning. Source lag, the second released proposal and bounded dose can break exact equality. I expect D and lateral to be close more often than random and lateral. Suite profiles can rotate an A-optimal choice, although inverse clipping, map rotations/reflections and changes in achieved motion may make this weak source prior ineffective on target. The next question after a robust independently derived advantage is its effect during genuinely live frozen-motor calibration and on native success.
