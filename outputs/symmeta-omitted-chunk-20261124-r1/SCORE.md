# Omitted-chunk visual scoring on unseen source tasks

## Registration

Registered on 24 November 2026 before inspecting this experiment's evaluation outputs. The reader worker owns the physical source collection, role/task manifest, reader training and exclusion audit. The question is whether prediction error on a third chunk identifies which of two yaws inferred from the other two chunks to trust, on Panda/Sawyer tasks unseen during fitting. All three chunks together contain 15 issued controls. The endpoints are the tuned Perceiver (T) and geometric SymMeta (G) at matched reader seeds 41–43. Wolpert and Kawato used forward predictions to determine contributions of multiple controllers. Here both readers face the same independently fitted visual reference. The earlier source-matched controller supplies the DIS pooling implementation. [Original proposal](../symmeta-yaw-blend-20261124-r1/RESULT.md) · [Wolpert and Kawato, 1998](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12662752/) · [Matched-source flow method](../symmeta-matched-flow-20261123-r1/OFFLINE.md)

1. For each chronological before/after RGB pair, pool DIS MEDIUM flow with the repository's fixed motion-magnitude weights. Map its unit 2D direction to the unit issued planar command rotated by the labeled yaw. Fit one real 2 × 2 matrix with zero intercept by weighted least squares on fit tasks only. Each fit task, physical trace within task, and scenario within trace carries equal mass at its level. Split a scenario's mass evenly across its directionally valid chunks. A scenario with no valid chunks contributes zero. Projection is independent of both readers. Only RGB and the issued planar command are needed at inference.
2. On chunk j, rotate its issued command by reader k's two-chunk yaw from the other original slots. Let E(k,j) be its unsigned shortest-arc error, in degrees, against the direction of omitted DIS flow mapped by the matrix. D is the mean of E(T,j) − E(G,j) over all three omitted chunks. A positive D favors G. The three leave-one-out estimates share observations. The inferential unit is the task.
3. On separate calibration tasks use the full three-chunk predictions. For weight w on G the output is T + w wrap(G − T), wrapped to [−180°,180°). Select the best constant c by minimum circular MAE on the grid 0, .01, …, 1. On directionally valid calibration rows compute the uncentered RMS s of D. Use w = clip(c + b D/s, 0, 1). Choose b from 0, 1/8, 1/4, 1/2, 1, 2 by the same full-population calibration loss. Ties choose the smaller grid value. Equal task/trace/scenario/seed weighting applies throughout. With undefined or zero s use c. A zero slope gives the calibrated constant exactly.
4. Require finite, nonzero commands and projected directions, valid physical chunks, and defined two-chunk yaws for all three folds. Invalid rows retain c. A missing G full yaw or an exactly antipodal full pair falls back to T for interpolation. Finite full T, positive finite tuned gain and valid tuned gripper sign are required for the deployment baseline. Keep its gain and sign on every row. No row is dropped from yaw MAE. For hard selection use the sign of D, breaking ties and missing scores with the more accurate of always-T and always-G on calibration (ties prefer T). Concordance includes full endpoint pairs with a unique better yaw, with its denominator separately reported.
5. Freeze prediction arrays, physical identifiers, fit and calibration parameters, input and code digests before opening the evaluation label file. Evaluate adaptive, T, G, fixed 50:50, the selected constant mix, hard selection, and the better-endpoint oracle. Report planar operator error with tuned gain. For the primary decision require the two paired 95% intervals for adaptive minus T and adaptive minus calibrated constant MAE to lie below zero, and the interval for hard concordance minus the calibration-selected constant selector to lie above zero. The fixed midpoint is an additional paired comparison. The boundaries are the matched competitors. A negative point estimate with an interval spanning zero does not establish a gain. An offline gain would support a fresh native test.
6. Use 2,000 percentile bootstrap draws, NumPy generator seed 20261124. Independently resample whole tasks with replacement in each of the fit, calibration and evaluation roles. Refit the projection, constant, score scale, slope and hard default inside each draw. Pair every method across the same test tasks, with all arms, original traces, scenarios and three reader seeds together. Also give test-task-only intervals conditional on the frozen fit. The joint intervals condition on the reader checkpoints and these source collections. Report role membership, counts, degeneracy, ties, and per-task/seed estimates. For a chance sanity check use whole-test-task sign flips of paired differences conditional on the frozen fit. The exchangeability assumption and small number of test clusters limit interpretation.
7. Qualify on Linux CPU with a synthetic known-answer example kept separate from the empirical run. In the real pre-label pass measure what changes if the omitted command rotates 90°, if the wrong omitted visual chunk is assigned, or if visual evidence is zeroed. Report changes in fold errors, score, mixing weight and emitted yaw. A calibration-selected zero slope or a shared endpoint can leave yaw unchanged even when scores move.

Task membership and exposure of original reader fits must be supplied by the reader worker before empirical inference. Reader-fit tasks may overlap the projection-fit role. Evaluation tasks and calibration tasks must be disjoint from both reader training and projection fitting. The estimator uses raw archived pixels and commands. Predictions and metrics from prior target panels do not enter the study.

The registered judge fit uses 224 earlier physical supports (eight tasks, two arms, two resets, seven shifts). Twelve tasks are reserved for training the two readers. Four calibration tasks and eight evaluation tasks use 224 and 448 separate physical traces respectively from the larger archive, with four resets per arm and shift. Task lists and grid counts are enforced in the scorer. Adjacent chunks share a physical boundary frame. When the middle token is omitted, its two endpoints can still occur separately in the retained neighbors. Omitted issued command, direct paired flow, and proprioceptive deltas are withheld by the reader mask. This design tests held-out motion-token consistency. It cannot establish generalization to wholly unseen pixels. [Reader collection and masking](INPUTS.md)
