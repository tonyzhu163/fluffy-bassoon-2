# Hidden embodiment from robot experience

Primary-source notes, 20 November 2026. **PACE is the closest arm-transfer experiment. ReGround is the closest frozen-VLA probe and bypass.** PACE includes Sawyer among its training arms and holds out both Panda and UR5e. ReGround keeps the visual scene and arm geometry fixed while changing actuation. Neither establishes that a reader trained on two arm families can identify a third from one practice attempt and improve a frozen PaliGemma motor. [PACE, §§III–IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.19141v1) · [ReGround, §§3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20244v1)

## Hidden body and dynamics

| Primary study | What the history identifies and what was trained | Quantitative scope and result |
|---|---|---|
| [PACE, 2026, §§III-C–IV, Table I](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.19141v1) | A moving window of Cartesian state, action and reward informs a task/context encoder. Training uses task labels for contrastive learning, next-state prediction and expert actions. The encoder is then frozen while a dynamics-aware controller is distilled. This is a state-based student with a trained motor. | 10 manipulation tasks × 5 source arms, 50 expert policies, offline expert data plus online distillation. Source breadth ablated at 1, 3 and 5 arms. Four held-out sim arms include Panda and UR5e. The five sources include Sawyer **and UR10e**. No verified trajectory total. The authors report 80% versus 60% on UR10e when training on the three lighter arms. |
| [Bayesian Meta-Learning for Few-Shot Policy Adaptation Across Robotic Platforms, 2021, §§IV–V](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.03697) | Holds the task sub-policy fixed. Five demonstrated joint-motion trajectories encode a 2-D platform latent and adapt a trajectory generator. Endpoints are matched across platforms. Target supervision therefore contains demonstrated motion and correspondence. | 100 geometry variants each of YuMi, Baxter, Franka and Kinova. The leave-one-family-out arm used **300 source variants across three families**. Authors judged held-out-family reaching unsatisfactory despite better results on new variants of familiar platforms. Five-shot within-family success is weak evidence for transfer to UR5e. |
| [DexFormer, 2026, §IV, Tables III–IV](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08278v1) | A jointly RL-trained grasp policy uses recent observation/action history without an explicit morphology ID. A shared 20-D joint layout and zero padding still convey structural information. The hands vary on a common Franka arm. | 300 training variants, 100 each from LEAP, Allegro and RAPID. Canonical hands and 32 additional variants per family tested. At the reported 40k-episode budget, five versus one history step raised mean grasp success from 73.0% to 77.5%, while reducing success on both RAPID test settings. |
| [DexFormer 2.0, Nov 2026, §§4.2–4.4, Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20273v2) | Jointly trained arm–hand policy receives explicit per-joint morphology descriptors and kinematic-tree information alongside 16-step memory. Removing memory tests its incremental value even with geometry supplied. | 4 arms × 6 hands, 18 pairings trained and 6 held out. Held-out stable grasp success was 51.18% with memory and 24.45% without it. The split tests new pairings of familiar components. |

## Sensorimotor probes and frozen policies

| Primary study | Evidence and training scale |
|---|---|
| [ReGround, Nov 2026, §§3–4, Fig. 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.20244v1) | Fits nominal and deployed forward dynamics from task-agnostic motor babbling, estimates delay and an action map, and tracks the frozen VLA’s intended path. This is target-specific system identification. Frozen π0.5 and SmolVLA, eight LIBERO tasks. On the 250 ms latency data sweep, **1,000 target transitions (50 s)** gave 0.98 success. At 400 ms, π0.5 rose from 0.16 to 0.90 (task-seed interquartile means). **Verified hard bypass** before the CUSUM shift alarm: the raw VLA action executes. The detector falsely activated in 2.1% of nominal episodes. The authors report that one-step tracking matched longer horizons on these shifts. |
| [Environment Probing Interaction Policies, ICLR 2019, §5, Table 1, Appendices B–C](https://arxiv.org/html/1907.11740) | A learned 10-step exploratory policy produces a latent rewarded for better held-out transition prediction. The downstream task policy is subsequently trained with that embedding. Striker uses one arm and 25 training combinations of object mass and damping. On held-out values the final miss distance was 0.162 m with the probe, 0.259 m with recent task history and 0.410 m with random interaction. Probe training used 200–400 updates × 10k steps. The task policy used 1,000 × 100k steps. Main comparison resets after probing. |
| [UP-OSI, RSS 2017, Algorithms 1–2, §IV-B](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss13/p48.pdf) | A universal policy learns with true simulator parameters. A separately supervised identifier predicts those parameters from state/action history at deployment. In the arm experiment the hidden scalar is the thrown block’s mass. The policy used 500 TRPO iterations × 50,000 samples. OSI used five rounds, each with 30 parameter draws × five seconds. A clean privileged-parameter control for our study. |
| [Embodiment-Classifier Guidance, Oct 2026, §§2–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22576v1) | Frozen π0 is steered by a source-versus-target classifier fitted to successful autonomous action chunks. Body labels and success are available during calibration. Four calibration tasks per suite, 2,500 VLA queries per task (about 23 target episodes/task on LIBERO-Object). Mean success across 12 controller/suite pairs rose from 72% to 80%. **On the speed-capped UR5e**, unguided was 84%, guided 77.5%, and simple smoothing 85.5%. This target-fitted classifier does not infer an unknown body from a new support trace. |

## What the earlier repository review already covered

- **VLRLA** trains a cross-episode memory bridge through a frozen action expert. RoboTwin used six seen and six unseen tasks on ALOHA–AgileX. The PPO history collector has 204,800 nominal chunk slots per 25-iteration run before termination masking. In the real two-color study, 169 training demonstrations from 185 episodes are recombined with feedback specifying a hidden *destination*. The separate fruit dataset had 391 episodes, six subtasks and 4,692 chunk decisions. These are distinct archives and units. [VLRLA, §5 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.19517v1) · [Repository review](../../docs/literature/frozen-vla-offline-icrl-review.md)
- **ICRT** pretrains on approximately 2,000 retained DROID trajectories after grouping/filtering a 10,000-demo sample, then uses 1,098 Franka trajectories across 29 tasks. One or more human demonstrations specify what to do in a scene with multiple possible tasks. The policy is trained for this objective and fixed at inference. Its sensorimotor prompt establishes task identity. [ICRT, §§III–V](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15980) · [Repository review](../../docs/literature/frozen-vla-offline-icrl-review.md)

**Decision for Panda/Sawyer → UR5e.** Count source arm families, distinct dynamics settings per arm, independent support/query reset pairs, and target calibration transitions separately. No reviewed result establishes a minimum number of trials for this frozen motor. Hold task and query reset fixed, swap matched versus wrong-body support, and compare against current observation alone and a simple action-map fit using the same probe budget. If correct history fails to beat swapped history on held-out UR5e, a gain over the raw motor cannot establish body identification. Measure nominal bypass by action equality at matched policy noise and by false activations per nominal episode.
