# What can a practice episode identify about a robot?

**For Panda/Sawyer → UR5e, train the reader to predict the effect of a command on a fresh trajectory.** A robot name can predict a source-average correction even if the reader ignores the interaction. A stronger test changes the command response in opposite directions *within each source arm* and asks whether swapping the practice reverses the selected correction. The earlier PaliGemma reader saw eight independent training trajectories (128 transitions) across two nominal arms. Resampling those transitions adds optimization steps but leaves the number of independent dynamics settings at two. [Source-data audit](../embodiment-meta-factory-20261120-r1/SCALE.md)

## What the original studies establish

| Study | What the history predicts and how it is trained | Scale, test and identification limit |
|---|---|---|
| **PEARL**, Rakelly et al. (2019) | Multiplies Gaussian factors from unordered transitions. The critic’s Bellman error and a KL penalty to a unit Gaussian train the encoder. The actor samples the resulting context. The released default uses state, action and reward, with next state enabled for randomized Walker dynamics. | Walker used **40 train / 10 test dynamics settings**, 200 steps per episode. Three continuous reward families used 100/30 each. Forward/backward tasks had two directions. Return on the same task family shows that context can guide behavior. The latent has no mass units or prescribed sign. [§§4–6](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/rakelly19a/rakelly19a.pdf) · [Appendix A](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/rakelly19a/rakelly19a-supp.pdf) · [Default](https://github.com/katerakelly/oyster/blob/44e20fddf181d8ca3852bdf9b6927d6b8c6f48fc/configs/default.py) · [Walker override](https://github.com/katerakelly/oyster/blob/44e20fddf181d8ca3852bdf9b6927d6b8c6f48fc/configs/walker_rand_params.json) |
| **VariBAD**, Zintgraf et al. (2020) | A GRU reads states, actions and rewards. The policy receives the inferred belief. Training decodes a full trajectory from a prefix, initializes the prior at a unit Gaussian and regularizes against the previous posterior. All MuJoCo runs used **only the reward decoder**, including randomized Walker. | Walker evaluation used the 50-million-frame checkpoint after later performance declined. Separate HalfCheetah rollouts for leftward and rightward rewards showed opposite latent signs and running directions. This establishes sensitivity to a **reward intervention**. It leaves open whether setting a latent coordinate causes a physical correction. [§§3, 5.2 and Appendices C.1, C.5–6, Fig. 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.08348) |
| **HiP-MDP**, Doshi-Velez & Konidaris (2016) | Learns shared basis functions for state changes given state and action. Transitions grouped by task instance teach the model without physical labels. On a new instance it updates a Gaussian posterior over the basis weights. Fixing the first weight and mask to one anchors the mean dynamics. | Acrobot used **eight training mass pairs** and evaluated a 16-pair grid that includes those training pairs. On Cartpole, the latent improved acceleration prediction across mass and length settings. The same balancing strategy worked throughout. Predictable dynamics alone do not establish a need for adaptive control. [§§3–5, Tables 1–2](https://cs.brown.edu/people/gdk/pubs/hip_mdps.pdf) |
| **UP-OSI**, Yu et al. (2017) | A universal policy trains with the simulator parameters. A history estimator regresses those parameters by squared error from recent states and actions (history length 3). Training includes trajectories collected when the controller uses an erroneous estimate. | Five estimator rounds each drew 30 parameter values and five seconds per draw. On a simulated double pendulum the authors set a **signed** center-of-mass offset from −0.6 to +0.6 m and plotted estimated versus true values, both rescaled to [−1, 1]. This is direct physical-sign recovery under that probe distribution. The study did not test a held-out robot arm or swap paired histories. [§§III–IV, Fig. 2d](https://www.roboticsproceedings.org/rss13/p48.pdf) |
| **RMA**, Kumar et al. (2021) | PPO jointly learns a controller and a teacher mapping 17 privileged simulator quantities to eight latent values. A student regresses that vector by MSE from 50 steps of proprioception, foot contact and actions. The teacher sets the target scale. The paper bins local terrain height and supplies the physical randomization ranges. | **1.2 billion** simulated steps for the base policy, **80 million** for the student. Real A1 payload and oily-surface tests support adaptation on the same body. Two latent components changed during slip and knee torque magnitude rose. That trace cannot assign either coordinate a friction value or establish the effect of reversing its sign. [§§III–V, Table I, Figs. 2–3](https://arxiv.org/html/2107.04034) |
| **Planar sliding**, Song & Boularias (2020) | Fits cellwise mass and friction maps to depth-observed motion under known pushes. A softmax of fit quality normalizes weights across candidate maps. The active probe can select the push on which candidate motion predictions disagree most. | Five pushes per object supported motion prediction. Under quasi-static flat sliding, different mass/friction pairs can give the same frictional force and diverge on balance at a table edge. Figure 3 reports **no clear motion-prediction advantage** from active over random pushes. A real KUKA also tested edge grasping on a hammer from ten poses. [§§IV–VI, Algorithms 1–2, Figs. 2–3, 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2008.01921) |

## Decision for the frozen PaliGemma study

1. **Establish the signed effect on source arms.** At matched starts, apply nominal and equal/opposite command-frame rotations. Record issued commands and measured end-effector displacements in one declared frame. Compare correct practice, opposite-sign practice, a common correction, raw actions and the known inverse. Score the signed transverse response and paired native successes rescued or broken. If one common correction works equally well, this setting offers no evidence that the motor needs history.
2. **Match the probe to the claim.** Free-space motion can reveal a command-frame error. For plate contact, pair pre-contact and post-contact windows and predict held-out object motion. If two settings produce indistinguishable public histories, report the shared predicted response and uncertainty. Fit per-channel scales on pooled source training data and freeze them, preserving physical zero and reporting errors in native units. Per-setting centering could remove the offset under test.
3. **Scale independent settings first.** At fixed transition and optimizer budgets, compare more layouts of the same settings with more independently varied settings on each arm. Split by entire support/query trajectories and hold out parameter values. The published budgets establish what those experiments used. They give no minimum pretraining count for a frozen PaliGemma motor or for a new arm family. Reserve UR5e for the frozen transfer comparison.
