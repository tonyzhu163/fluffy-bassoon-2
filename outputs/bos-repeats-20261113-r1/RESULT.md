# BOS repeats released and RoboCasa PPO lineage checked

On November 13, 2026 Shanghai time, seven audited BOS repeats totaling 3.02 GiB were released. Six pairs were byte-identical. The seventh was a superseded OpenDrawer qualification tar whose surviving tar recovers the same 332 files. Both tar hashes and both identical member manifests were rechecked. All seven surviving objects passed full SHA-256 before release and remained present after release. The replacement map was published to BOS and read back before deleting any object. [Exact paths, hashes and timestamps](RELEASE.json)

The older 1.41 TiB five-task Flow-SDE PPO campaign finished November 4. Its RLinf rollout stored the mean of ten denoising-step critic values, while PPO replay used the selected step's critic value. This put different values on the two sides of GAE and PPO value clipping. Unchanged-weight actor log probabilities matched on the audited production batches. The collected actions, rewards and measured endpoints remain evidence of the implementation that ran.
