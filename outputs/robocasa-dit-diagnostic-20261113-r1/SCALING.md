# RoboCasa source expansion

Use RoboCasa for the next source-data experiment. The completed AD, DPT, DIT and IC-IQL comparison used OpenDrawer, CloseToasterOvenDoor and CoffeeSetupMug. Switching to RoboTwin would also change the simulator, motor and task distribution and would not isolate whether more RoboCasa learning histories improve these transformers.

The concrete next coverage is the existing three tasks plus OpenCabinet, SlideDishwasherRack and PickPlaceCounterToStove. Preserve CloseFridge and TurnOnMicrowave as held-out tasks for this comparison. Do not silently replace Coffee with Faucet: Faucet belongs to a different proposed teacher inventory, not the completed transformer corpus.

The three new pilots have each completed ten PPO updates and 1,280 training episodes. Their paired endpoints were 38→40, 42→41 and 40→40 successes out of 64. These establish working collection, not improved teachers. Extend the saved complete training states in fresh run identities and retain trajectories across updates, native rewards, task/case boundaries, policy versions and intermediate evaluation checkpoints. Set the extension length from measured throughput and a concrete approved reservation. Do not commit to an arbitrary update count or call flat endpoints effective new teachers.

Use the corrected selected-step critic implementation. The original three-task corpus was collected with the older critic-value mismatch, while the new pilots used RLinf `8f9bfa76`. Keep the old data as the fixed baseline for an initial three-versus-six corpus comparison, recording that the added data also comes from a corrected collector. That experiment measures the effect of adding these sources; it cannot attribute any gain solely to task count. A comparison intended to isolate task diversity should regenerate the original three with the corrected collector too.

Before training all four algorithms, explicitly collect and verify DPT teacher-query actions; behavior rollouts do not supply these labels. Hold the architecture, training split, logical batch, update budget, initialization seeds and evaluation cases fixed across the algorithms. Refit any corpus-dependent Q/V estimators on the declared training split. Balance task sampling and state whether total updates or updates per source are held fixed.

Report both task success and the paired effect of retaining history, on ID and OOD tasks. Finish the first-attempt diagnostic and compare matched-budget AD and DIT before attributing the current DIT lead to in-context adaptation. A better endpoint alone supports a better policy; it does not establish a benefit from prior episodes.

No new GPU job is launched or reserved here. The prior closed campaign retains conservative commitments of 162.129/170 historical GPU-hours and 48.835/50 all-new GPU-hours. CPU inspection uses no GPU-hours. A teacher extension and transformer retraining package needs a separate concrete estimate and an explicit increase if it exceeds these existing hard caps. A larger campaign is not implicitly charged to a fresh allowance.
