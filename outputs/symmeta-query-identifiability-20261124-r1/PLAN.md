## Problem and competing explanations

PR7 found a median ratio of second to first command singular values of 0.099 on its relabeled in-family supports. Its general 2×2 and axis-swap errors could concern a direction the motor rarely uses. Alternatively, the early approach might sample one line while subsequent manipulation needs another. A fixed three-direction intervention previously worsened five-control 3D motion forecasts on every target arm. Task-guided experiment design motivates asking which directions subsequent commands use. The linear-system result does not establish an exploration rule for a nonlinear robot policy. ## Decision

Consistent additional weak energy on independent late commands with appreciable requested offsets would weaken the unused-degree-of-freedom explanation and motivate testing whether a short task-directed intervention can learn that response. Query fractions comparable to support, tiny absolute offsets, or an apparent effect confined to selecting the support's own weakest axis would favor passive geometry or a narrower question. Compare with independent early commands and wrong-task directions and discuss task heterogeneity without a chosen numerical cutoff. The study can establish demand in command space. Efficacy of a new estimator or intervention requires different evidence.
