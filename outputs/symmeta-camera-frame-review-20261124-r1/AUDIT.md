## Does the spatial baseline use calibration?

A third fit, C, kept B's image, flow, validity, CNN, parameter count and camera/interface exposure and clamped both its global H and local counterpart-grid H to identity throughout training and inference. I matched its sealed budget and all draw hashes to B and joined every held-out row to B's observation and native label. On digital primary rolls C scored 6.77° versus B's 6.10°, a paired excess of +0.67° [+0.01, +1.33]. On genuine physical orbits C scored 26.69° versus B's 16.52°, a paired excess of **+10.17° [+8.70, +11.72]**. Explicit calibration therefore helped the raw reader substantially on real viewpoint changes. The result also fits a visual shortcut on digital views, though it cannot attribute that shortcut specifically to border shape. B's remaining 8.13° physical gap to A survives this stronger demonstration that B can use its calibration.
