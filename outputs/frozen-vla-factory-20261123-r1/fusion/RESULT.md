# Registered FK gave the lowest yaw error under camera rotation

On 64 held-out simulated UR5e × frozen π₀.₅ supports, adding joint kinematics to the seed-41 visual reader reduced error on every support at both quarter rolls. Adding vision to registered FK increased error on all eight task means at both rolls and upright. For a robot with qualified encoders, geometry and policy-frame registration, use FK alone for this yaw estimate. These data support a visual fallback when kinematics are unavailable, particularly with a familiar upright camera. Its absolute error under unknown camera orientation remains large.

Intervals use 20,000 paired whole-task resamples (seed 41), conditional on these eight tasks, layouts and fixed transforms. Repeated views share episodes. The common 1.4× zoom follow-up gave fusion MAEs of 7.07°, 3.61°, 2.63°, 4.80° and 8.18° from −30° through +30° in 15° steps. FK remained at 2.02°. The crop itself raised upright visual MAE to 26.07°.

**Confidence and failure mode.** Each branch aligns the three issued XY means to its own three motion vectors through the summed dot and cross products. Vision retains the frozen reader's chunk weights. World motion uses equal chunk weights. Refit after omitting each chunk and measure the jackknife angular variance on the tangent at the full heading. The visual fraction in the circular mean is the world variance divided by the sum of the two variances. This parameter-free measure of stability cannot detect a fixed error common to all chunks. Under the *synthetic* intervention rotating only the measured FK vectors by −30° / +30° with upright pixels, the median visual fraction stayed 0.19. FK then scored 30.61° / 29.39°, fusion 23.60° / 21.42° and vision 6.83° in both. Vision helped misregistered FK, but trusting the steadier branch still left substantial bias. Independent evidence of registration is needed before choosing which frame to trust.

**Physical viewpoint check.** Eight fresh simulator replays on two tasks repeated the archived frozen motor's three mean commands, five times each. The camera orbited the same moving scene while actions and physics stayed fixed. At world azimuths −30° / 0° / +30°, visual MAE was 23.73° / 7.76° / 34.22°, fusion was 4.37° / 3.97° / 2.12° and the contemporaneous world grip-site sensor gave 1.83° throughout. Fusion's excess over that sensor at −30° / 0° / +30° was 1.17° / 2.05° / 0.38° on Long 5 and 3.93° / 2.24° / 0.22° on Spatial 0. These are descriptive per-task effects.
