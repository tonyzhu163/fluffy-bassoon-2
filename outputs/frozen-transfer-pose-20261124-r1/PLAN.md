## Question and evidence

Can maintaining the wrist orientation requested by the frozen task policy prevent Kinova3 from reaching the requested physical grip point on its failed transfers? A successful local full-pose FK witness rules out static joint infeasibility at that pose to the reported residual. Failure of a local search is inconclusive. Distinguish a static exclusion, a one-control hardware speed limitation and a target the native impedance controller simply tracks imperfectly.

RelaxedIK trades pose matching against other motion objectives and bounds joint speed. Task Space Regions specify permissible translations and rotations in task frames. Neither paper supplies a permissible tilt for these grasps. Use unchanged full orientation first, then unconstrained orientation as an optimistic geometric comparison. Report degrees paid and absolute position attained. The legacy 1 mm and 0.01 rad are reset/readback conventions, with no status as grasp tolerances.
