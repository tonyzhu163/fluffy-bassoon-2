# Abstaining on small planar corrections also suppressed every gripper-only correction

With the source-fitted gate, both frozen readers left all eight unshifted UR5e supports alone at each of three seeds. The same rule also left all eight gripper-only shifts alone at every seed, undoing every correct gripper inversion. Across the remaining 80 shifted supports per reader, the rule preserved every original estimate. The largest evidence against using this whole-correction rule comes from the gripper-only shifts. The planar error objective cannot distinguish those cases from a nominal interface. [Registered plan](PLAN.md)

When the estimated planar transformation fell inside that radius around identity, the rule restored the full identity correction, including the gripper sign. Always issuing the identity had planar errors of zero on unshifted supports and 1.3832 on shifted supports. The small mean improvement in the shifted rows came entirely from removing planar corrections on the gripper-only supports. Keeping the original gripper decision when the planar system abstains would preserve all observed gripper decisions on this panel. Its closed-loop behavior requires its own evaluation.
