# Can public registration rescue the frozen PR7?

Registered 2026-11-24 before running this worker's first prediction. The comparison concerns passive interface inference on UR5e after training on Panda and Sawyer.

## Problem

A stronger result from a camera-trained reader could come entirely from registering the observed images. The original PR7 Perceiver already predicts yaw accurately at its native view. Its large errors under exact quarter turns establish sensitivity to raster orientation. The historical physical archive also contains independent camera movement, for which a single plane cannot recover disoccluded or off-plane content. ## Hypothesis and prior

My prior favors recovery at digital rolls with correct public calibration. Recovery for both physical orbit signs is less certain. Support for the wider claim would require gains over raw frozen inference, modest remaining error relative to the same physical trajectory upright, and little further gain from the matched source-trained readers. A lower task-paired error for refitted A or spatial B on the independent physical views would weaken the case for reusing PR7. An interval spanning zero alone cannot establish equivalence.
