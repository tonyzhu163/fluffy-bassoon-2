## What changed at contact

Only sliding friction on four finger collision geoms changed, after the standard reset settling. Setting it to 0.05 produced a **realized finger-pad/bottle coefficient of 0.95**, down from 2.00 on the same active geom pairs. Under identical nominal actions, the bottle paths first differed by over 1 mm at control 44, reaching a maximum gap of 1.77 mm. The transport tape also had identical actions and a 27.48 mm maximum gap. Regrasp used the same public-state feedback controller in both branches. Its actions first differed at control 45, after finger contact at control 40. Its 35.25 cm maximum bottle gap therefore includes the feedback response.
