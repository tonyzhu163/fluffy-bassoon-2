The r3/r4 artifacts retain real chronological action-expert PPO behavior at the H50 decision cadence. They support a future direct-action history dataset after export/feature qualification. They do not yet constitute an admitted improving teacher or a common matched frozen-motor dataset for every AD/DPT/Vintix formulation. This audit read actual saved payloads on allocated Linux CPU; it ran no model, simulator or extra evaluation.

AD can use the actual `(observation, executed action, reward, boundary)` sequence with behavior-action targets after a RoboCasa exporter and feature contract are qualified. The existing generic `rlinf_native_ppo_data.reconstruct_rows` is a RoboTwin trace reader with 14-D/reset-ID assumptions and is not a ready reader for these 12-D RoboCasa `environment/` files.

DPT-style direct-action query labels remain absent. They require a preselected, independently qualified final teacher queried offline on the saved decision observations with bound checkpoint/config/norm/sampling seeds. Historical executed actions and feedback must remain unchanged. No such model queries were run in this audit.

The same observations can feed a Vintix-style causal decoder, but this collection is not fixed-teacher, annealed bounded-noise CND. Its action expert changes during actual PPO learning with constant configured Flow-SDE exploration. A CND comparison needs its declared history construction and targets.

For the matched frozen-motor residual formulation, a prospective bridge is the full teacher's action minus the frozen motor's action on the same observation and recorded sampler noise, in the correct pre-binary coordinates. Frozen-motor counterpart predictions, residual bounds/targets and reproduction qualification have not been generated. Initial-noise inversion, KV/velocity adapters and candidate-selector targets each need their own compatible labels and qualifications; the saved learned-expert Gaussian cannot simply be renamed a frozen-motor steering action. Full-weight/action-expert PPO remains the reward/learning positive control and reference, while matched residual PPO is a separate source route.

The retained complete banks are separate from the interrupted old iteration-7 prefix. Its 1,600 resolved controls are preserved in raw chunks, while an additional 800-control reservation is conservatively charged without a complete recovered trajectory. Do not silently include that partial prefix as a complete training bank or claim every charged control is a lossless history row.
