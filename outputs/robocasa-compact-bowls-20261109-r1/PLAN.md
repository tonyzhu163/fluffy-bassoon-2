# Can frozen π0.5 perform both explicit goal skills?

The previous one-fruit pilot had no released placements on query scenes. Before fitting a reward-conditioned selector, center the plate in front of the arm and place the bowls symmetrically 30 cm to either side at the original plate depth. Retain the original counter, assets, fruit positions relative to the plate, object height and orientation, robot reset, and camera views. Alternate bowl identity by seed parity. Only reset setup and evaluation use object state. The motor sees normal RGB, proprioception and the explicit skill instruction.

Prospective geometry-only admission: in increasing seed order from 961000–961015 admit the first eight passing the seven checks in layout.py (four development then four fresh). Do not inspect policy performance for selection. All dishes must remain supported, separated and stable within 2 cm of their requested pose after 12 neutral control steps. Both bowls must start empty, all four fruits on the plate, and the horizontal arm-origin distance to each dish at most 72 cm. These are geometry checks, not proof that the policy can reach the goals.

On each admitted development seed, run both explicit prompts with matched physical state, policy inputs and Gaussian noise tapes, 10 denoising steps, five controls per replan, horizon 500 after setup. Report the first grasp, first fruit inside each bowl, and first native in-bowl plus hand-far (>25 cm) event. Stop symmetrically at any released placement or timeout. Record the original four-fruit task success separately. Report the counts by requested and observed side. Qualification requires that both requests succeed on the same seed at least once and that correct releases outnumber wrong releases over the panel. A fail closes the motor check; do not read hidden-goal outcomes on fresh scenes.

If qualified, enumerate one support prompt and two conditional query prompts on two development pairs. Fit to maximize support + query reward; tie-break on support, then fixed action order. Freeze the best constant continuation on the same development data. Execute both motor alternatives on each scene of two unseen pairs. Apply both hidden preferences to the same support and query traces; record binary-reward, reversed-reward and memoryless counts with pairs as sampling units. These small counts qualify a mechanism, not a generalization claim. GPU limit: one Pro6K for up to 60 minutes.
