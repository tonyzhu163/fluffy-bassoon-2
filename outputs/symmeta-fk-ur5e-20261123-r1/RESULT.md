# Trusted kinematics and learned visual yaw on UR5e

Baige job `job-pc0ioesm8tle` completed the registered 96-pair π₀.₅ rerun at commit `3fe381d60aa035eff4cdc29daba532b7d8caf841`. Both methods used the seed-41 gain and gripper estimate. Replacing the learned visual yaw with yaw derived from measured joint motion and trusted forward kinematics yielded 78/96 successes versus 76/96 for the learned yaw. Seven pairs favored kinematics, five favored vision and 84 tied. The difference was +2.1 percentage points, with a 95% task-bootstrap interval from −2.1 to +7.3. On the 64 held-out cases the counts were 52 versus 53 for a difference of −1.6 points (interval −4.7 to 0.0).

All 96 per-case files had the scheduled indices and identical reset receipts, released prompts and initial policy proposals within each pair. The all-case and held-out counts were recomputed from the native outcomes. The job exited zero after 1,101 seconds on two allocated GPUs (0.61 launcher GPU-hours). The fresh visual run matched the historical overall count, with four individual outcomes changing. The lower offline angle error from known kinematics produced little measured change in native success on this panel. This experiment does not establish equivalence.
