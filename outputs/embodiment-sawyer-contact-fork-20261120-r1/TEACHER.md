## Native state and controls

At construction the adapter observes the plate, goal and current gripper surfaces from the already advanced arm. It reads the same fields before every issued action. After a native Euler integration step, the last solver positions can precede the endpoint coordinates. The adapter reconstructs the position stage from current native coordinates in private MuJoCo data. The live solver, contacts, OSC state, episode clock and control counter stay under the runner's ownership. Both branches use this endpoint convention. Historical bank collection read robosuite's position cache directly. The reset branch therefore also measures this precise live adapter on each layout. [Original geometry reader](../libero-paligemma-crossarm-20261120-r1/sim.py) · [Endpoint reconstruction in the original contact observer](../embodiment-sawyer-source-contact-20261120-r1/geometry.py)

The existing handoff rule evaluates two disposable controllers against that one scene. It starts in approach by default. It chooses descent when lateral approach error is below 30 mm, angle error is below 0.32 rad and the lowest surface is within 131 mm above the table. It chooses push when the surface is within 13 mm above the table, the angle error is below 0.32 rad and either approach or push lateral error is below 30 mm. Push takes precedence. The chosen controller starts with fresh timers and integral. Its alignment probes issue zero controls. Contact triggers the fork in the runner. The teacher uses geometry to select its phase, including when a brief touch has ended by the control boundary. [Handoff implementation](../libero-paligemma-dualarm-20261120-r2/recover_teacher.py)

If first touch is observed during control `k`, the learner has already spent that entire control. The first replacement is control `k + 1`. Both continuations receive at most `300 - k` further issued actions and stop at native termination or truncation. The reset teacher receives at most 300. Raw success can end the archive before the horizon. The teacher may use the remaining native budget even after the raw branch's earlier stopping time. Score the native terminal success and reward. A no-touch or already terminal prefix has no actionable handoff. Keep it visible alongside invalid replays and report its status separately from an attempted teacher failure. ## What the three branches identify

- Report rescues among all 30 raw failures, losses among all 20 raw successes and the paired net change on the 50 starts. For a complete panel, net change in percentage points is `100 × (rescues − losses) / 50`. Show reset-expert success separately for each of these outcomes. Keep missing or invalid pairs and their reasons visible against the original denominators.
- A rescue from an identical prefix demonstrates one successful alternative within that start's remaining controls. A lost raw success demonstrates the cost of replacing a continuation that already worked. The intervention replaces every subsequent command, so a gain alone cannot identify which command or contact feature caused it.
- A weak reset teacher leaves a failed handoff compatible with a limited controller. A reset success paired with a handoff failure still mixes inherited state, the phase rule's ability to resume, and the controls already spent before touch. Report the touch index, available controls, controls used and how each branch ended. Horizon failures establish failure within budget. Time to success beyond that horizon is right-censored, and early native successes shorten their own trajectories.
- This is a privileged simulation diagnostic on the frozen motor's development layouts. A rescue establishes feasibility for that state. It supplies neither a deployable trigger nor a public-observation policy. Keep these forks out of training. A corrective-demonstration claim would need separate training layouts and evaluation of a policy using the observations available at deployment.
