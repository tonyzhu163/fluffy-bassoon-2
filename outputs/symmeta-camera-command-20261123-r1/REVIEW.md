# Independent readback of paired optical-roll and kinematics results

The frozen seed-41 SymMeta reader confounded displayed image roll with command yaw on held-out UR5e. The three reports join to the same 96 passive trajectories by task, command transform and support-file hash. On the 64 out-of-distribution (OOD) trajectories, native yaw mean absolute error (MAE) was 6.83° upright, 101.23° at +90° and 84.52° at −90°. Positive roll means counterclockwise on screen, opposite the physical camera-roll sign in the render check. Mean paired prediction shifts were −97.49° and +88.26°. Every trajectory across all eight tasks shifted in the opposite direction to the displayed roll and incurred greater error at both signs. Adding the known displayed roll to the predicted yaw still left 25.87° and 27.78° OOD MAE, whereas undoing the pixel rotation restored every prediction exactly. The issued commands and independently reconstructed world motion identified yaw on all 96 trajectories. Forward kinematics used archived joint increments in radians to recover world displacements in metres, with at least 53.78 mm of planar travel per trajectory. OOD yaw MAE was 2.02°, ranging from 1.10° to 2.97° by task. All eight nominal active replays exactly reproduced archived joint and end-effector deltas at recorded precision, although all eight full-state reset hashes differed. With identical planar readout weights, SymMeta beat the source-calibrated brightness-flow baseline by 5.58° upright, but its advantage varied by task under roll, averaging 2.01° better at +90° and 0.59° worse at −90°. The modest-roll follow-up supports directional sensitivity on these same trajectories. Against its own 1.4× zoomed upright baseline, mean OOD shifts were −20.12°/+17.89° at +15°/−15° and −39.95°/+27.51° at +30°/−30°. The paired positive-versus-negative roll contrast had the expected sign on all eight tasks at both magnitudes. Upright counterrotation controls had mean absolute shifts of 8.76°–9.63°, so resampling itself materially affects individual predictions. Relative to the zoomed baseline MAE of 26.07°, neither 15° sign increased aggregate error. Both 30° signs increased it, on seven of eight tasks each.
