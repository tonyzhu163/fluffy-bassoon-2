applies inverse rotation privately. It then calls the actual student sampler,
using delta_v=clip(gain*((x_t-target)/t-v_native),-1,1) at each native Euler

is exactly0. Extra native target queries are teacher-only and explicitly counted.
This controller tests capacity of the intervention, not learned CTM competence.

training-overlay scenes for objects0/1. Maximize training success and prefer
the greater gain on ties. No held-out gain tuning. Then compare selected tracker,
zero bridge and external labels on the exact held-out scene/reset/noise plans
from the prior interface screen. Preserve every candidate and raw outcome.

policy weights, fresh recurrent state, scene/reset plans and native-noise seed.

changed outcome or divergent trajectory blocks the provisional capacity pass.
Count the six additional repeated-control episodes separately in the run budget.

These operator diagnostics remain separate from the public-history reader inputs.
