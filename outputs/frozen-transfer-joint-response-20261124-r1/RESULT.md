# Five recent joint responses reduced Kinova3's next-step position error by 0.067 mm

A Panda-fitted joint servo model predicted Kinova3's next 50 ms of tool motion to **0.169 mm** mean error. Updating that model from the last five Kinova3 controls reduced error to **0.102 mm**. The paired reduction was **0.067 mm** (95% interval **0.046–0.088 mm**, conditional on the two sampled tasks). Using five earlier controls gave **0.162 mm**. Recent history beat both comparisons on each of the six trajectories. The average measured step was 5.09 mm. ## What the comparison changes

The mean reduction against the source model was 0.085 mm on bowl onto stove and 0.049 mm on bowl onto plate. The reduction against stale history was 0.061 mm (conditional 95% interval 0.037–0.084 mm). Source-side cross-validation selected the per-joint model and 0.1 ridge weight in all three held-layout folds. The same update on held-out Panda layouts reduced error by just 0.0034 mm, from 0.0413 to 0.0380 mm. The pooled Panda fit remained close to the per-joint fit on Kinova3. Current velocity alone predicted substantially more of the motion than instantaneous setpoint geometry. ## Implication for a frozen-policy comparison

The measurable headroom is about 1.3% of the mean target displacement. A later comparison can test whether it changes decisions near contact. Freeze the same VLA, use each branch's own live observations, and pair native resets, proposal noise, timing and gripper commands. Give a tuned geometry-informed Perceiver and the structured response model identical training tapes and the same per-decision current q, velocity, tool Jacobian, joint limits, frozen proposal and five time-stamped native command/encoder transitions. For a direct comparison, give the Perceiver per-joint tokens, axes and a residual head on the source PD prediction. Train it across multiple source bodies, select width, depth, learning rate, training duration and regularization on held-out source bodies and tasks, then freeze it. Hold out the test arm, tasks and fresh layouts throughout selection. The six diagnostic tapes are development evidence.

Both readers should forecast motion for candidate actions under the same bounded action solver and the same source-validated physical motion reference. Compare them with unchanged current-pose IK, a history-free source response and stale history. Qualify matched opening actions, encoder timestamps and legal commands, and measure paired native reward, rescues, losses, time to success, saturation and prediction error. The previous feedback experiment lost four native successes while improving its local forecast. Native reward therefore decides whether this one-step improvement warrants an adaptation claim.
