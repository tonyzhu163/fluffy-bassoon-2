# Frozen adaptation on a new robot arm

The decision is whether an adapter can complete more tasks than a strong frozen policy and its tuned history reader on held-out embodiments. The policy weights stay fixed. Each comparison specifies any source training, target observations, separate calibration or demonstrations, added inference, and whether the command interface was artificially shifted. Task success means the simulator's native terminal reward. New tests are exploratory until replicated on fresh starts.

## Evidence that shapes the experiments

- **Dynamics and command interpretation.** ReGround fits a model of the target dynamics on motor interactions and tracks the source policy's intended motion. ICBA trains a policy and a transformer on command–response context across controllers. CAPABLE uses joint histories and kinematics to train a capability-conditioned residual around a frozen policy under actuator faults. These results motivate testing which response information changes decisions while keeping track of extra training and sensing. [ReGround](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20244) · [ICBA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39280) · [CAPABLE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11971)
- **Arm appearance and object motion.** Mirage renders the source arm into the target observation and tracks the resulting source policy motion. Im2Flow2Act learns to express a task through object flow and then to act from that flow. Their mechanisms suggest separate interventions on what the policy sees and on whether an object responds when the gripper moves. Our earlier source-arm rendering changed all ten checked policy proposals but solved the same one of two drawer starts as the native view. That comparison covers one task. [Mirage](https://robot-mirage.github.io/) · [Im2Flow2Act](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v270/xu25a.html) · [Earlier source-view trial](../frozen-vla-source-view-20261123-r1/NATIVE.md)
- **Timing and plan execution.** UMI matches inference latency and uses relative trajectories. PACE selects replanning boundaries from speeds in the proposed action sequence. BID samples action chunks for temporal consistency. A distinct hypothesis is that the *measured motion of the new arm* tells us where it is along the previous plan. A comparison against ordinary temporal averaging at the same policy-call frequency isolates the added information. [UMI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.10329) · [PACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00537) · [BID](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/0d78dd998f7b9ac79604d47a2d79bb0d-Abstract-Conference.html) · [Action Chunking with Transformers](https://tonyzhaozh.github.io/aloha/)
- **Choosing a correction with very little evidence.** Wolpert and Kawato proposed using forward prediction errors to weight inverse controllers. In our archive the geometric and learned yaw estimates have complementary errors, but their equal-weight blend changed pooled held-out yaw by only −0.34° (95% task interval −1.05° to +0.45°). This motivates a direct test of whether each candidate predicts motion in one omitted chunk of an ordinary, passive trace. [Multiple paired models](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12662752/) · [Archived comparison](../symmeta-yaw-blend-20261124-r1/RESULT.md)

## First comparisons

| Question | Decision-changing experiment |
| --- | --- |
| Can three passive chunks select a better yaw estimate? | Fit an independent visual motion judge on source tasks. Compare a weight based on omitted-chunk error against the tuned reader and constant weights on held-out source tasks. Hold the tuned gain and gripper outputs fixed. If the score predicts a lower yaw error beyond the fixed comparisons, freeze it for native comparisons on fresh layouts with a matched-inference ensemble. |
| Do delays or slow responses transfer across bodies? | Compare a causal temporal predictor or correction with a static response and suitable timing control on recorded motions from genuinely different arms. Check whether the needed signal is present at deployment. |
| Does object motion tell the frozen policy when an interaction worked? | Isolate a public cue for a physical interaction, test it across independent arms and starts, and compare it against commanded gripper state, robot motion or time as appropriate. Simulator internals can supply labels but cannot be adapter inputs. |
| Does measured progress improve execution of the frozen action chunk? | Compare native rewards for the raw policy, tuned Perceiver retry, fixed temporal averaging, and otherwise identical averaging aligned to observed motion. Use the two simulated arm substitutions, all eight released task identities and matched fresh starts. Log rescues, losses, and whether the intervention actually changed a command. |

## Follow-up on wrist pose feasibility

The geometry study found that one demonstrated wrist pose missed a Kinova3 bowl-release position by at least 25.1 mm on drawer layout 14. A 6.53° tilt admitted finger contact at the same position. The frozen policy failed earlier in the sequence. RelaxedIK trades motion constraints in generating feasible poses, and task-space regions describe acceptable sets of end-effector poses. The cheaper question for our policy is whether its own proposals actually encounter the same constraint. A separate worker will replay the issued commands across arms and compare reachable translational error at the requested wrist orientation and with relaxed orientation against the observed tracking error. A near-zero correctable deficit would deprioritize a kinematic wrist adapter even if human demonstration poses benefit. [Earlier geometry and native contact evidence](../frozen-vla-reachability-20261123-r1/RESULT.md) · [RelaxedIK](https://roboticsproceedings.org/rss14/p43.html) · [Task space regions](https://publications.ri.cmu.edu/storage/publications/pub_files/2011/10/dmitry_ijrr10-1.pdf)

An improved motion prediction or label fit cannot by itself establish a native-success advantage. An unmodified arm on a nominal interface, an arm substitution on a nominal interface, an imposed command shift and a causal change in camera or contact sensing answer different questions. The run records identify which comparison each result supports.

## Follow-up on grasp identity

In the registered wrist-motion trial, hand opening perfectly ordered the two branches within each of the 146 opposing carry/empty observations. The natural failure that may need a visual reader is holding the wrong item with a perfectly ordinary jaw opening. Before fitting such a reader, count definite non-target pickups on the original policy tapes, using the instructed object identities and sustained physical retention. Distinguish a wrong approach without pickup and allow legitimate multi-object sequences. This follow-up reuses the prior panel and is exploratory. [Wrist-motion evidence](../frozen-transfer-object-20261124-r1/RESULT.md)

## Follow-up on mixing two readers

The held-out source yaw test favored a fixed mix over the per-trace choice. To isolate whether the geometric reader adds information beyond a second Perceiver, compare two independently seeded tuned readers against one tuned and one geometric reader. Each ensemble uses two reader predictions and retains the same base gain and gripper output. Use fixed midpoints and, separately, fit each method's weight on the same source calibration split. Test on the four original held-out arm–policy archives and report the prior inspection of those archives.

The native phase comparison uses index five as the fixed control. The adaptive index can only move earlier. Any favorable native result would justify a fresh test against an observation-blind control with the same marginal index distribution, before attributing the gain to the measured motion itself. The original 64-episode panel is unchanged.

## Native test of a visually timed subgoal

All three failures on the earlier untreated target tapes involved the drawer and bowl. The earlier camera reader, trained on Panda demonstrations, distinguished open and closed drawers better than an elapsed-time clock on crossed Kinova3 replays. Fixed tapes established that the one-replan placement cue reached the policy interface. They could not establish whether the motor used it to solve the task. SayCan grounds skill selection in feasible actions, while VoxPoser turns language into spatial objectives. Here a small test can isolate a single link in that argument. On four new released layouts with two target arms, compare the frozen motor, the tuned retry, and two otherwise identical tuned retries that issue the same placement cue at the time chosen by vision or by the source clock. Score native task reward. [Existing camera monitor and delivery test](../frozen-vla-phase-transfer-20261123-r1/RESULT.md) · [SayCan](https://say-can.github.io/) · [VoxPoser](https://voxposer.github.io/)

## Can another body's successful trajectory supply a missing action?

In the closed-loop drawer test IIWA solved layouts 36 and 37 under the original instruction, and Kinova3 failed on both. RAM retrieves an affordance demonstration and transfers its contact geometry. DITTO warps demonstrated object trajectories to the new scene before execution. A cheap preliminary question is whether a successful donor's already-issued Cartesian controls suffice on the other arm when the exact same scene is available. The registered comparison uses the target's own commands to check reproduction, commands from the other body in the same layout, and commands from that body in a different layout. This directly tests raw trajectory reuse. A null leaves both feedback tracking and object-relative retargeting open. [RAM](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v270/kuang25a.html) · [DITTO](https://ditto.cs.uni-freiburg.de/)

## What a learned joint-space baseline would need

The Panda joint-control trial suggests conditioning on current posture when translating a frozen Cartesian policy into native joint actions. Cross-morphology neural inverse kinematics already exists. GGIK represents a robot geometrically as a graph, and MorphIK learns to propose configurations for unseen kinematic chains. An informative new comparison would give the Perceiver and the proposed adapter the same measured posture, kinematic description and action limits, lock both before the held-out body, and score the frozen policy by task reward through the target joint controller. The earlier Panda comparison used a fixed fitted inverse. [GGIK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.01902) · [MorphIK](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29908) · [Panda evidence](../frozen-vla-native-controller-20261123-r1/RESULT-NATIVE.md)

## The source approach and live policy handoff

Johns separates approaching an object from interacting with it. A learned visual estimate guides the robot to the handoff pose, then it replays demonstrated interaction velocities. The present experiment assigns the demonstrated movements to the approach and lets an existing frozen vision-language-action policy close the loop on the target body for grasp and placement. The known idea of splitting a task into phases limits a novelty claim based on the split alone. The empirical questions are whether the source approach adds successes across arms and tasks and whether a learned command correction applied to exactly the same demonstration changes that result. [Coarse-to-Fine Imitation Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.06411) · [Source demonstration and handoff trial](../frozen-transfer-demonstration-20261124-r1/PLAN.md)

## Camera pose as a competing explanation

Jiang et al. condition behavior-cloned robot policies on calibrated per-pixel camera rays. They find that policies can infer camera pose from static backgrounds and that explicit calibration improves held-out viewpoint performance. Mirage also uses calibrated camera geometry to transfer a fixed policy across robot bodies. These results make a calibrated learned Perceiver a necessary comparator to explicit reference-view resampling. Our question is whether the latter improves a separate command-map reader under jointly varying camera and control frames while the original task policy stays frozen. Target-only image roll reversal tests arithmetic. Rendering a changed optical viewpoint adds effects such as parallax and occlusion. [View-Invariant Policy Learning with Camera Conditioning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02268) · [Mirage](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19249)

## How to combine identified and unidentified command directions

Task-optimal exploration relates experiment design to the later control objective. ALPaCA learns a feature map and a prior for online Bayesian linear regression. Bayesian meta-learning across robotic platforms also uses a probabilistic model to adapt from a small number of target trajectories. These precedents make the narrower research question worthwhile here. With a frozen policy and 15 controls, does a geometry-informed combination of the direct reader and a physical fit beat the better component and a source-tuned scalar combination on held-out bodies? A direct reader that already used the same target measurements cannot be treated as independent prior evidence. [Wagenmaker et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/wagenmaker21a.html) · [Harrison et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.08912) · [Ghadirzadeh et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.03697)
