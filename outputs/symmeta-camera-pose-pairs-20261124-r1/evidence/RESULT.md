# Physical validation

The 72 unique trajectories contain 360 real native view tapes. The full panel's ±30° orbits gave 0.66 px mean post-reset tool-endpoint registration error at either sign using the registered plane. Every one of the eight tasks improved over identity at both signs. The task-cluster intervals for whole-image RGB change included zero. Physical ±20° optical rolls gave endpoint residuals at numerical precision and mean RGB error below 2.9 levels. [Complete results, uncertainty and prior update](../RESULT.md)

- The compiled models use 45° vertical FOV and perspective projection. At every reset, three known noncollinear world points were rendered into a separate diagnostic scene for each physical pose. Their observed centroids established the column and row signs, focal scale and pixel-center convention independently of tool endpoints. Across 1,080 projected marks, median error was 0.026 px and maximum was 0.095 px in recorded coordinates.
- Every native TCP projection was in bounds, the integration-state readback passed around each paired render, and the measured nominal frame and native TCP agreed on the simulator observable clock. The nominal calibration returned the original image exactly. Across all views, the largest absolute elementwise residual of the normalized inverse homography product from identity was 6.8 × 10⁻¹⁵.
- Every original input and native-view NPZ digest matched the bank. Public controls matched across paired views, the stored motion map's first two channels matched the DIS tensor exactly, and the masks contained three five-step chunks. The eight historical paths reproduced all native array values and camera poses on the original three views. [Release checks](release-check.json) · [Bank](reader-bank.json.gz)
