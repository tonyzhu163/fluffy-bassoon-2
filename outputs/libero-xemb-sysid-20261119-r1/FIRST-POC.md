Registered before the first motor smoke produced an outcome. The original 48-pair motor gate and 96-trial reader evaluation retain their registered criteria. This smaller engineering pilot asks whether an independently estimated interface can rescue a matched live retry. It also scores a closed-form visual alternative. A positive pilot licenses replication across tasks.

Use seven single-factor fitting conditions on LIBERO Goal tasks 5 and 7, resets 0 and 1. Freeze seed 41 after 1,500 updates per reader. Use the preregistered native Goal 1 development task, support reset 11 and different query reset 13. Evaluate the nominal interface, the withheld yaw-180/scale-0.65/inverted-grip composition, and the unseen 45-degree yaw. Use the same 15 controls for active and passive support. Record current-prefix controls against the live query horizon. Keep the frozen motor, physical starts and motor randomness paired. Archive labels supervise training factors. The test-time reader receives only public observations and actions.

For the narrowest proof of concept, at least one shifted case must reach the native goal with a factored public-history reader where nominal, cleared and wrong-donor controls all fail, and the oracle must succeed on the same case. Compare analytic proprioception and a train-calibrated image-motion estimator on each case. Record joint, non-equivariant and online controls to distinguish mechanism and memory. Report every attempted condition including BASE and any ties. A tie with closed-form visual calibration leaves the need for learning open. A failure of an oracle or reader is a different result.
