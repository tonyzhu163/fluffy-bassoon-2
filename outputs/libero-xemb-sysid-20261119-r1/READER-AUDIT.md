## Repairs and evidence

- **Matched support cost.** The former default exposed three active chunks and up to 24 passive chunks. Both collection defaults now stop at 15 controls. The shared tape loader admits the first three five-control chunks from either mode and checks the valid prefix against actual executed controls. A seven-control attempt contributes five and two controls, followed by padding. Older, longer passive archives retain their original acquisition cost. Live outputs distinguish acquired support, support observed by the reader, online query probes and query motor controls. A cleared arm pays for the support it discards. An online prefix spends the query horizon. [Collector](collect.py) · [Live accounting](eval_live.py)
- **Signal contract.** RGB stays uint8 on disk. The reader maps bytes to unit RGB once and accepts the motor’s existing unit-float online input. Learned wrist and jaw features use centimetres. Analytic fits use metres and weight issued commands by the executed fraction of each chunk. Invalid chunks are masked before feature extraction. The offline report logs observed motion in millimetres, absolute RGB change in byte units, valid/padded counts and the exposure histogram for both splits. [Readers](../../src/fluffy_bassoon/xemb/readers.py) · [Trainer](train_readers.py)
- **Frame zero and pairing.** Collection compares the active and passive reset images, states and physics before a mode can overwrite the other’s identity. It checks across embodiments as well, including the passive opening proposal and noise-bank digest. The loader verifies the stored frame-zero digests against the actual tape and its first image. The frame-zero probe accepts just reset RGB and the eight-state vector. The live query seed depends on task and reset and is shared across factors. The motor callback compares public reset data, noise and prompt before acting. All immediate-motor arms also check the opening proposal. Donors form a permutation within the same task, reset and seed, with a different embodiment at every position. [Collector](collect.py) · [Reader](../../src/fluffy_bassoon/xemb/readers.py) · [Live evaluator](eval_live.py)
- **Fitting and stopping.** Archive admission checks the complete scheduled identities, labels, task splits, seeds and unique tape paths. The optimizer receives only fitting rows. Calibration uses nominal fitting tapes. Development scores are computed after each fixed final fit and never choose update count or checkpoint. Fitting scores help distinguish underfitting from a development transfer failure. The full offline audit retains every model and seed even when the compute gate fails. A missing, nonfinite or at-least-20° mean continuous-angle yaw error cannot authorize full live evaluation. A failed requested gate writes the result and exits 4. Full live admission checks the completed audit, archive digest, registered updates/seeds and the measured gate. The distributed sequence separately enforces native Gate 1. [Split](../libero-meta-learning-20261117-r1/SPLITS.json) · [Trainer](train_readers.py) · [Distributed sequence](distributed.sh)
- **Inference integrity.** Flax serializes layer lists as state dictionaries. Live restore now reconstructs lists against the configured architecture and checks the mode, model, seed, final step, archive digest and input schema before inference. Checkpoints retain optimizer and sampler state. Earlier weights require refitting because their motion units differ. [Checkpoint writer](train_readers.py) · [Loader](eval_live.py)
- **Analytic comparisons.** Proprioceptive yaw comes from measured command/displacement alignment. The nominal gain calibrates translation scale and the jaw–command correlation estimates polarity. The inexpensive visual alternative solves brightness constancy on moving pixels, fits a camera projection on nominal public fitting traces, removes measured vertical motion and contracts recovered planar motion with issued XY. It uses the proprioceptive scale and gripper estimates. A rank-deficient calibration records the reason the visual comparison is unavailable. Native camera motion, contacts and scene-specific projection can degrade this estimator. The training-prior and nominal scores make the frame-zero control interpretable under the registered imbalance.
