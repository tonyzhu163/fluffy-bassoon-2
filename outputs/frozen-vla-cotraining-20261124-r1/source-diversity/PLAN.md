# Can the source arm be varied independently of the frozen policy?

This exploratory check is separate from the registered Panda fit. The R2 Goal 5 archive contains candidates generated by a privileged plate-pushing teacher on the simulated Sawyer. Inspect native training receipts, tape payloads and scene identities. An arm label on a rendered image or a reward on a policy rollout cannot establish who supplied its commands. Count complete teacher trajectories separately from recovery suffixes and validation.

The archived state stores a signed finger separation. Stock OpenPI consumes two individual finger measurements. Replay the first generated training seed, 300000, under its original Sawyer physics and exact expert commands once, with both cameras and actual finger sensors. Save a self-contained sample on Baige if it completes with native reward. Compare against the original search tape, archived state and reward. Failure would leave the archived recorded successes intact and the full-input replay unqualified. No target-arm simulation, reserved reset, policy inference, fitting or GPU. One Baige CPU core.
