# Native arm transfer on Panda fitting tasks

Registered before looking at evaluation outcomes or choosing a task or layout from the data. The parent panel changes both arm and task exposure. Here the instruction and fitted weights stay fixed while the robot changes from Panda to Kinova3. Which differences among stock, policy-only, adapter-only and joint training carry across arms on familiar tasks? A joint gain limited to Panda counts against transfer. Equal outcomes with the joint history suppressed weaken evidence that access to history improves native success.

**Task selection.** Within each of libero_object and libero_spatial, take the lexicographically first distinct normalized English instruction among Panda train trajectories contributing at least one training example. Normalize by lowercasing, replacing underscores with spaces and collapsing whitespace. Record all candidate instructions, selected training trajectory IDs, example counts, source manifest digest and unique released BASE BDDL mapping. Check actual training coverage against the fitting schedule. Held-out-only trajectories and endpoints have no role in selection.

**Starts.** Within each selected BASE task, take the highest two released row indices after excluding any identifiable matching scene starts in either staged source split. Use a documented map of object/fixture state or source reset identity when available. A demo or shard ordinal, different seed, arm, or raw image hash alone cannot establish different scenes. If no defensible mapping exists, retain the highest two rows, disclose possible overlap and make no fresh-layout claim. Once locked, use both rows on both native arms. Use seed 42820000 + 10000 × suite index (Object 0, Spatial 1) + 100 × released task ID + row. Use the parent's stock paired diffusion bank derived from that seed.

**Execution.** Eight physical cases, each with original, policy_only, adapter_only, joint and joint_zero for 40 terminal episodes. The zero control uses the same joint weights with all 52 history entries zero at every replan. All modes share native observations, nominal OSC, five issued controls per replan and the official success/horizon stopping rule. Pair released row, scene, instruction, horizon and noise across robots; pair raw opening state and inputs within robot. Use the parent's immutable fit and native runner. No new fitting. A capped, deterministic actuator fixture qualifies the real simulator and runtime contracts on at most two Baige CPU cores. It contributes no policy endpoints. Parent submits within 1.5 GPU-hours and its remaining 12 GPU-hour aggregate. Keep partial jobs labeled incomplete.

**Readout.** Preserve all outcomes by task, row, arm and condition. Within each arm report native success, native return, joint-minus-each-comparator paired differences, rescues and losses. For each shared task/row report the Kinova3-minus-Panda difference in those paired effects. With four shared starts, report all four contrasts and leave-one-start-out sensitivity; task and layout variability cannot be estimated reliably here. An improvement on matched tasks alone does not establish held-out-task generalization. Public stock pretraining exposure and any unidentified demonstration layout overlap remain outside the split guarantee.
