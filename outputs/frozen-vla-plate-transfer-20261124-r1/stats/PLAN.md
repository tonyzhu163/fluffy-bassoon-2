## Counts, tests and uncertainty

At each eligible look, report the four cells (both succeed, both fail, MIXED-only rescue b, comparator-only loss c), arm success counts and rates, and signed paired risk difference (b−c)/N. With d=b+c discordances, under equality and iid pairs, b conditional on d is Binomial(d, 1/2). Use the inclusive two-sided exact p-value min(1, 2 Pr[Bin(d,1/2) ≤ min(b,c)]); p=1 at d=0. Compare the raw p with that look's incremental spend, not the sum spent so far. An equivalent across-look adjusted p through the looks examined is min(1, min_j 0.05 p_j / a_j). The sign of the first crossing determines direction, permanently.

Run the secondary test only after a positive primary crossing. When that gate opens, examine the secondary at *all* already-complete scheduled prefixes in increasing N, then at each future permitted look. Record its earliest crossing and N when authorized to claim it. If primary benefit crosses but secondary has not crossed, continue until a secondary crossing or exhaustion. A significant primary loss stops the study. A secondary loss after primary benefit resolves attribution against MIXED; two positive crossings support a source-mixture benefit for this matched source design. Unresolved comparisons never establish equivalence. Exact single-look size plus a union bound controls each contrast at 0.05 across looks. Under both nulls an erroneous rejection requires rejecting the primary. If only the secondary null is true, a false secondary claim requires crossing its own predeclared rule at some look, even when the primary gate is data-dependent. This is strong fixed-sequence familywise control.

For simultaneous 95% effect intervals over both contrasts and every registered look, divide a_j equally between contrasts, then equally between q=Pr(discordance) and s=Pr(MIXED wins | discordance). Use equal-tailed exact Clopper–Pearson bounds for d of N and b of d. When d=0 take s in [0,1]. When q=0 the effect is zero for any s. Evaluate q(2s−1) at all four rectangle corners to bound the risk difference. Per-parameter two-sided failure probability is a_j/4, and union bounds across all four components and all looks give joint coverage at least 95%, including at the selected stopping look. Conservative intervals may contain zero at a test crossing; decisions follow the registered test. Provide the gated contrast's counts and interval descriptively when its test is closed.
