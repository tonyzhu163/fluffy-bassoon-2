## Frozen parameters and controls

The fits used the pilot's 52→16→320 bias-free head and differentiated native Pi0.compute_loss directly. Only 5,952 adapter scalars could change per condition. Both saw 192 source windows over the same 16 generated layouts, 384 paired draws per epoch, eight epochs and 96 Adam updates at learning rate 0.001. The mixed fit replaced six of each layout's twelve Panda windows with Sawyer windows at corresponding phase slots. Mean training loss from epoch 1 to 8 was 1.49058 to 1.13421 for Panda-only and 1.31377 to 1.00361 for mixed. The conditions have different training targets, so the comparison between adapters uses the common validation set. ## Execution and handoff

The original Baige Distributed Training submission `job-2x8nf6npvz1z` spent 32 allocated seconds and exited before the launcher. The scheduler had concatenated two commands. Its replacement `job-vnk0801b5ywq` on one RTX 4090 spent 835 launcher seconds. The shared filesystem returned ENOSPC while opening the temporary optimizer file after printing step 61. The last complete checkpoint contained both heads and their Adam states at step 60. [First command receipt](first-job-command.jpg) · [Interruption and recovery cursor](evidence/gpu02/recovery.txt)

The sealed job `job-acm6is0ht2ee` used one distributed RTX 4090, restored that checkpoint and completed through step 96 in 690 launcher seconds. Restore preserved the first 60 training records and the step-61 draw permutation. The replayed step-61 loss was higher by 0.0000621 for Panda-only and 0.0001857 for mixed relative to the failed process's printed values. Cross-process numerical replay was therefore inexact. Only the durable step-60 state supplied weights and moments to the recovery. Total recorded time across all three attempts was 1,557 seconds, or 0.4325 GPU-hours, using scheduler time for the first and launcher time for the latter two.
