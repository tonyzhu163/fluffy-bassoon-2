# Do the compact-bowl branches provide usable action labels?

The three native PPO teachers improved the memoryless adapter. DPT failed the own-versus-donor test on matched retries. This next test follows the compact-bowl positive control in fable.md. The question is whether one completed support placement can tell a frozen student which physical actions to take on a fresh query scene. The hidden rewarded side is balanced, the prompt is identical, and the side that the support controller probes is chosen independently of the hidden side. A timeout remains in the denominator. Scoring uses native fruit-in-receptacle and gripper-far predicates for a custom one-fruit goal.

## Qualify the labels

Use the first eight geometry-admissible fresh layouts starting at seed 987100, before reading any policy outcomes. The previous four development kitchen layouts are excluded. Use the existing seven geometry checks, one neutral frozen Human300 π0.5 pickup prefix per layout, noise seed equal to scene plus 501003, ten denoising steps, five controls per replan and at most 500 delivered controls total. Branch the existing privileged carry left and right after the first grasp. Replay the whole prefix in fresh environments and check the physical state and raw public observation at the fork. Save delivered actions, actual execution length, public RGB and proprioception at query replans, and the outcome of each branch. Omit a training label for timeout or a release into the wrong target. A missed pickup counts as two failed alternatives. Report all eight, two-sided coverage, per-target labels, the oracle ceiling, and the best fixed continuation under balanced hidden rewards.

Advance if at least five of eight layouts permit both target-specific releases and the per-goal oracle beats the best fixed continuation. Otherwise inspect the recorded failures to decide which part of the actuator or horizon limited coverage. One measured repair on new prespecified layouts may be justified before another qualification. Keep candidate selection and changes on development data.

## Fit and evaluation after qualification

Use disjoint scene pools for fitting, development and confirmation, with both latent values crossed against the same query appearances. Keep original public images and state, completed support actions and rewards, and only executed target controls with their masks. Privileged poses, the hidden goal and the other candidate outcomes are reserved for teaching or scoring. Compare the matching support, a support with the opposite hidden goal, a reward-masked or flipped support and separately trained memoryless LoRA. First measure whether a public-history-only decoder can identify the goal when support completes and whether the same observation alone can do so. Score the learned motor's actual releases and recoveries on fresh scenes, with incomplete supports retained. This is a binary positive control. It cannot establish generalization to a continuous latent, a new task or unmodified native tasks.

Use Baige Linux resources for execution, and log the actual device and time. Budget the positive-control pilot within 12 elapsed hours and the repository's 50 GPU-hour allocation limit. Preserve checkpoints and artifacts for the separate native retry-collection workstream. If the label gate fails, use the remaining time on the matched first-contact CloseDrawer diagnostic already in fable.md.

For a passing qualification, take the first 24 geometry-admissible seeds from 987200 for training, eight from 987400 for checkpoint selection and 16 from 987600 for confirmation. Geometry and split membership are independent of task success. No confirmation scene enters training, normalization or checkpoint selection. Pair distinct scenes within each split. Assign the support probe side from a balanced permutation independently of both hidden-side labels, then score its executed trajectory under each side. Identical physical query starts occur with both hidden goals. In the eight-scene diagnostic, leave out both scenes in each pair, including their support and query appearances. Keep all timeouts.

A target chunk begins at a pre-action public observation and contains at most the next 50 delivered commands. Mask after the last delivered control. Canonicalize the two binary command coordinates using the gym wrapper's threshold, then apply the checkpoint action normalizer. For matched-state contrasts use the final common five-control boundary preceding the first differing left/right action. Identical RGB and proprioception at that boundary must hold. Drop an ambiguous target when two continuations reach the same rewarded bowl. Keep abstentions in coverage counts. The first closed-loop learned-carry test starts every condition at the same verified frozen pickup; separately report success per admitted scene and per grasp. This conditions policy deployment on an evaluator-selected decision phase.

For the pilot fit, train rank-eight action-expert LoRA and DPT + LoRA with the same batch of six masked targets, optimizer and flow draw. Half the eligible draws use the final shared observation before the two teacher commands first diverge. Compare 128, 512 and 1,024 updates. Select memoryless and own-history DPT separately by the number of physically successful hidden goals on the eight validation scenes. Break ties by lower masked validation error at the matched decision, then earlier update. Freeze selection before collecting learned-policy outcomes on the 16 confirmation layouts. Frozen and memoryless policies are goal blind and their single physical trajectory can be scored under both balanced hidden goals. An own history with counterfactual reward is the same public support with only its observed goal reward changed. A different-scene donor uses another scene's completed attempt at the opposing hidden goal, with the same support probe side. Every condition gets the same noise schedule at a given query. Report a masked-reward input separately.
