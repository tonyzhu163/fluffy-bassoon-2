A full 3D matrix beating a planar matrix and raw action in closed loop would support a cross-axis motor correction. If the target succeeds raw on the selected fresh starts, these queries measure preservation and the pilot has little headroom. If Panda also fails, that cell provides no evidence of a failure caused by the target arm. A successful privileged source-trajectory tracker with failed raw target shows that this target arm can accomplish the task on that scene and motivates isolating visual/proprioceptive conditioning from motor response.
