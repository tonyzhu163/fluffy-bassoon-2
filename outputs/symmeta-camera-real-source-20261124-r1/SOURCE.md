# What the new source bank measures

The genuine source observations came from 192 independently indexed Panda/Sawyer frozen-motor supports. Each of 24 tasks contributes both bodies and four source command maps per body. There are 128 training trajectories on 16 tasks and 64 validation trajectories on eight disjoint tasks. The feature cache contains 1,152 distinct training image sequences and 320 distinct validation sequences. All 977 eligible digital training trajectories also exclude the eight validation tasks. [Exact real IDs](source-ids.json) · [Digital IDs](evidence/cache01/digital-ids.json) · [Geometry readback](evidence/cache01/geometry-summary.json)

Every training trajectory was replayed with its archived frozen motor's successive five-control means. The changing camera shared the simulator state and capture time with the nominal view. Across nonnominal training examples the camera center moved 0.137–0.881 m. Its measured plane H shifted the central image pixel 5.85–32.80 px. The raw true views are fully observed at every angle. The corresponding projected nominal images lack an average of 16–17% of pixels at ±12°, 29% at ±24°, 46% at ±42° and 50% at ±49°. Independent eight-pixel edge masks leave 87.5% of the raw observation. All four edges and the all-valid condition occur at every pose and tape. This changes the validity cue while preserving the same H and source control sequence. [Per-pose and per-mask geometry](evidence/cache01/geometry.json.gz)

Training-set tool projection error averaged 1.77 px before and 0.76 px after plane registration. On held-out source tasks the corresponding errors were 1.76 and 1.33 px. These summaries use the three post-reset boundaries. The largest independent raster landmark error was 0.059 pooled pixels. For the failed first attempt, two landmarks overlapped at wide azimuth. The repaired diagnostic places three visible noncollinear markers in the actual camera frame and retains the independent raster check. [Complete per-trajectory renders and diagnostics manifest](evidence/render02/source-records.tar.gz) · [Nine-view fixture](evidence/render02/Panda-0540/row.json)

All fresh public motor reset vectors passed the collector's 2×10⁻⁶ tolerance. As in camdata, none of the fresh full simulator state fingerprints matched the historical collecting host. The median/max absolute discrepancy in archived versus fresh chunk joint increments was 0.0053/0.0300 rad. The final nominal RGB mean absolute discrepancy was 1.10/3.26 byte levels (median/max across tapes). Physical and synthetic replacements share the exact fresh trajectory. This tests passive reading on four specific training magnitudes of horizontal orbit, two source bodies, 128 physical training motions and a fixed reset plane. It does not isolate padding from nonplanar appearance and newly visible surfaces. The identical replay also permits an image-space check. On the shared one-pixel-eroded projection footprint, we compared actual versus plane-projected nominal byte RGB on the same four boundaries. Mean absolute differences were 0 levels nominally, 21.69–22.34 at ±12° and 39.20–45.13 at ±49°. These include changes from depth, appearance and interpolation.
