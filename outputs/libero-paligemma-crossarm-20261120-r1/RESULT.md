# PaliGemma source-arm simulation result

The frozen PaliGemma policy with correct UR5e practice succeeded on **0/2** fresh Goal 5 starts. Raw PaliGemma, cleared practice, wrong-arm practice and privileged calibration also scored **0/2**. All ten runs reached the 300-control horizon with zero terminal reward. Every controller saw identical opening images, state, instruction and motor proposal within its paired start. This single-task pilot established that the reader can change a frozen policy's commands. It found no closed-loop transfer gain. [Paired native report](evidence/motion01/dev/report.json) · [Scheduler receipt](baige-result.jpg)

## Training and information flow

1. We loaded the complete general `google/paligemma-3b-mix-224` checkpoint used by the original fluffy-bassoon pipeline. Its three weight shards matched the pinned publisher identities. A fresh action head and rank-eight LoRA in the final two Gemma layers learned from simulated Panda controls. The successful collector supplied 11 Panda episodes in 16 attempted poses. Nine poses trained the motor and two selected update 150 of 300. Its selected held-out mean absolute action error was 0.0349 in normalized controller units. It succeeded on 0/2 independent Panda task starts. [Checkpoint provenance](evidence/assets01/weights.json) · [SFT manifest](evidence/panda01/motor/manifest.json) · [Source native report](evidence/panda01/panda_native/report.json)
2. The reader learned to predict measured responses to new commands from 16 prior command–motion transitions. It trained for 100 updates on Panda and Sawyer. On separate source pose 28, normalized prediction error averaged 1.092 with the correct body's history, 1.097 with the other body's history and 1.555 with a zero embedding. [Reader checkpoint](evidence/reader01/pretrain/reader-stage1.pt) · [Stage 1 readout](evidence/reader01/pretrain/report.json)
3. The planned two-arm expert-imitation experiment stopped because the collector obtained 0/16 successful Sawyer demonstrations. A separate diagnostic jointly trained the reader and a seven-axis action adapter for 150 updates. Both source arms contributed measured responses and actual frozen PaliGemma proposals. The loss asked the adapted command to produce the motion predicted for the original Panda command under measured source response models. The reader turns 16 interactions into a 16-dimensional vector. The adapter uses it to generate an affine correction for each action in the five-action chunk. This conditions the output of the frozen VLA. The motor's checkpoint SHA-256 was identical before and after joint fitting, with zero trainable motor parameters and zero motor gradients. [Source bank](evidence/gpu01/demos_motor/index.json) · [Joint readout](evidence/motion01/joint/report.json) · [Joint reader and adapter](evidence/motion01/joint/reader-adapter.pt)

On the cached fit proposals, the correct-history Sawyer motion MSE fell from 0.1174 to 0.0366 under the fitted source dynamics model. Replacing the history with the wrong source arm gave 0.1073. These are in-sample model predictions. On UR5e, the correct-history first Cartesian command differed from the raw command by RMS 0.181 and 0.032 on the two starts. Both changes were physically executed. The native reward was zero for both. [Joint fit](evidence/motion01/joint/report.json) · [Executed actions](evidence/motion01/dev/report.json)

## Embodiment identification

We tested the same eight command-matched histories used by the prior pilot. An offline diagnostic assigned each embedding to the nearest mean of two labelled reference histories per arm, including labelled UR5e references supplied solely to the diagnostic. The pretrained reader classified 3/8 histories correctly. After joint training it classified **2/8**, including **0/4 UR5e**, 2/2 Panda and 0/2 Sawyer. This test provides no evidence for reliable arm identification. A continuous latent could still encode response properties that this classifier misses. [Distances and individual predictions](evidence/identity01/report.json)

On a separate archived 16-command UR5e query, the pretrained reader had normalized MSE 2.420 with UR5e practice, 2.418 with Panda practice, 2.419 with Sawyer practice and 2.829 with zero context. The two support poses used command-matched scripts and the same query. [UR5e dynamics readout](evidence/devmotion01/report.json)

## Why the primary experiment stopped

The native source demonstrator reproduced all 120 published Panda tool observations exactly on the original pose and seed, with terminal reward 1. On one diagnosed Sawyer start, link 4 collided with the wooden cabinet while the gripper remained above the plate. A planner found a cabinet-clear tilted approach in scratch kinematics. Executed through the native controller, the corrected approach reached the plate and moved it 6.9 mm. It still earned zero reward. This observation explains one source failure and leaves the other starts unclassified. [Panda replay](evidence/demoqual03/demos/index.json) · [Sawyer collision trace](evidence/demoqual06/run.log) · [Clearance attempt](evidence/demoqual08/demos/index.json)

Failure on both fresh Panda motor starts limits what these target zeros tell us about adaptation. The next discriminating experiment is a source policy that reliably solves the native task on fresh starts of both arms, followed by the same paired transfer controls. The two UR5e starts are development diagnostics. The reserved IIWA and the physical robot were never evaluated.
