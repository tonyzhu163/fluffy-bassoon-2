# Source-only PaliGemma control in simulation

Can 16 measured practice transitions improve a frozen motor's native success on an arm family excluded from all robot-action training? The first, single-instruction test is LIBERO-Pro2.0 BASE Goal 5, pushing the plate to the front of the stove. It holds the task fixed across arms. All arms use the native end-effector Cartesian interface and a common gripper. Source models are Panda and Sawyer. UR5e is development. IIWA is the reserved morphology. Images, actions and robot states come from simulation. Physical testing is deferred.

Initialize the complete general PaliGemma VLM from the original fluffy-bassoon pipeline, with fresh action weights. Record the exact model and revision. Collect successful scripted on-body demonstrations without using a robot-pretrained policy. Train the motor on source arms, qualify the frozen motor on independent source starts, and pretrain the reader and jointly train the reader and residual adapter on source interactions and demonstrated actions. Keep the VLA frozen during both reader stages. Compare correct history with empty, wrong-body, unmodified and supplied calibration controls on paired fresh starts. Evaluate native task rewards. A loss on one source arm makes cross-arm comparisons uninterpretable. Failure of privileged calibration to rescue development starts argues against benchmarking this task/adapter on the reserved arm.

Run three independent implementation workers for on-body demonstrations and simulator support, the PaliGemma motor, and the reader and evaluation. The coordinator owns integration, cluster execution and reporting. Use Baige's Distributed Training queue, including for rollout collection. Use at most 20 GPU-hours in this exploratory allocation. Retain intermediate evidence and diagnose recoverable failures before proceeding. Start from 64993b92 and fluffy-bassoon 905edb15.
