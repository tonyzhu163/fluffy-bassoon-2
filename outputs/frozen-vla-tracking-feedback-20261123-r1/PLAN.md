At the VLA's five-control cadence, the law predicts observed tool translation from current and preceding native commands. Use the prior measured residual to predict the next unexplained residual. First remove source command-dependent error with a source-only regression on current and previous commands. Its online variant receives the immediately preceding remaining residual; its negative control receives a stale residual. Compare entirely out of sample by physical arm on source rollouts and on independent unmodified Kinova3 and UR5e rollouts. Fit a scalar persistence coefficient from source data only.
