# What would justify scaling embodiment adaptation?

**Decision at base revision `29f1db55` — defer scaling and keep UR5e sealed.** Two hypotheses warrant a source-only simulation test. H1 is that measured motion selects between corrections whose native task benefits reverse across hidden settings. H2 is that the learned gate preserves frozen-motor success on nominal settings and gains successes where intervention helps. The independent Panda/Sawyer SFT workstream owns motor qualification. ### 1. A weak comparator can make harmful history look useful

**Measured.** On the original Goal 5 plate push, PaliGemma scored 0/2 on fresh Panda starts. All five UR5e controllers scored 0/2. In the separate two-task π0.5 pilot, correct history beat wrong history 2/4 to 0/4, yet lost two starts to raw and rescued none. In an eight-task friction replay, even knowing the true material tied fixed default at 14/16. The latest raw SFT source screen on 50 layouts per arm reports Panda/Sawyer counts of 25/6 for update 1600 and 34/20 for extended regression. Neither clears the registered 38/50 per-arm development gate. **Cheapest H1 screen, proposed.** After source qualification, cross two hidden source settings with four fresh layouts of one task. Identity and two prespecified corrections on the same frozen PaliGemma checkpoint cost 24 closed-loop queries. Require a reversal of the successful correction on at least two layouts and a net native advantage for the setting-aware rule over every fixed choice. Stop this correction menu if it fails. On independent layouts, challenge the reader with a source-fitted, history-blind adapter using the same residual head, current inputs, data and fit budget. Include a fixed mean correction and raw identity as candidates. Empty input to a history-trained head can be out of distribution. Credit H1 only if correct history beats raw, the best source-selected history-blind alternative and matched wrong history.

### 2. An arm swap can change the scene, the information or the budget

The existing Goal 5 protocol matches opening state, motor noise and first proposal, cycles eight 16-control supports over 100 queries, and gives analytic calibration extra target pulses. Keep the opening checks. Give all primary branches identical support actions, resets, simulated time, policy-call allowance, action bounds, query horizon and replan stride. Raw and fixed branches discard the measured response. Charge shared practice once per declared reuse period. Also allow raw to spend the same total action/time budget on unassisted attempts and report native success by that deadline. [Transfer protocol](../libero-paligemma-dualarm-20261120-r2/LARGE-TRANSFER.md)

Cross the same two dynamics settings within a visibly identical source arm, holding task, layout, probe commands, camera and controller convention fixed. Swap only measured responses at query time. Separately change camera or support layout while keeping dynamics fixed. Keep robot/config IDs, filenames, seeds, joint-count masks and privileged simulator fields out of learner inputs. A static image/current-only control that matches history, or a gain that follows the nuisance swap, defeats the motion-specific explanation. Use source settings for these decisions. Lock weights, thresholds, probes, support assignment and analysis before the single UR5e evaluation. Only the fixed reader may consume public target practice. Keep all fitting, including the earlier protocol’s pulse calibration, on sources. Prior UR5e pilots are historical exposure. IIWA remains reserved. ### 3. A pooled success rate can pass an always-abstaining gate

**H2 decision, proposed.** Register nominal and correction-needed strata on source development before confirmation. Verify that forced identity reproduces raw actions on identical inputs. For the learned gate require **both** a paired 95% lower bound above −5 percentage points versus raw on nominal layouts and a lower bound above zero versus raw and the locked history-blind comparator on correction-needed layouts. Also require the H1 wrong-history contrast there. Report interventions, abstentions, rescues and spoiled successes on *all* registered starts. Always abstaining gives zero rescues. If any bound misses its gate, withhold the transfer claim. An interval spanning the threshold leaves the effect unresolved.

**Precision.** For one fixed task, body and support bank, let `g` and `l` count gains and losses among `n` layout pairs. The effect is `(g-l)/n`. With the repository’s conservative paired 95% interval, zero discordances give a lower bound of `0.0125**(1/n) - 1`, or −8.39 points at 50 pairs and −4.29 at 100. At 100, zero gains and one loss give −6.21 points. Twelve gains and two losses give +10 points with interval [−2.12, +21.06]. These are design calculations. The five-point tolerance is a proposed maximum acceptable nominal loss. Size each stratum separately using source discordances before unsealing. [Reproducible interval function](../libero-paligemma-dualarm-20261120-r2/large_transfer_stats.py)

One hundred Goal 5 layouts on UR5e measure one task on one target morphology. Reused support tapes, correction branches and training seeds add no independent robots. Report each task and body separately, retain the layout pairing and give worst-case bounds for missing starts. Claims across tasks or kinematic families require independent held-out units at those levels. **Review cost was 0 GPUh.** Only local reading and arithmetic were performed.
