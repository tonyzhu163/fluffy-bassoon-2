## Proposed pilot and decision rules

All counts and thresholds below are **design choices**. A condition is a robot family under one specified dynamics or command setting. Conditions on a common robot share its kinematics. Layouts replicate a condition, and windows from a tape share a trajectory. Count families, conditions, layouts and tapes separately.

**1. Does condition breadth improve prediction on withheld dynamics at fixed interaction cost?** Start with the existing Panda/Sawyer simulator, independent of SFT. Apply nominal commands, +90° yaw, −90° yaw or translation gain 0.65 on both arms. Vary the command mapping while measuring wrist motion in a fixed world frame so yaw is observable from the reader inputs. Narrow bank A takes nominal and +90°. Bank B takes −90° and gain 0.65. Hold out an entire yaw level (+45°) and a conjunction of familiar levels (+90° with gain 0.65) across all fits. Score these blocks separately. These are interface changes on two physical models. A related π0.5 control with a changed wrist frame gave oracle 47/48 and raw 9/48, while a physical finger-offset oracle scored 22/48 versus raw 23/48. Neither result qualifies a new motor or arm. [Interface control](../libero-xemb-sysid-20261119-r1/RESULT.md) · [Physical TCP control](../libero-xemb-tcp-20261120-r1/RESULT.md)

| Equal-cost training bank | Families × settings per family | Separate support + query resets per cell | Tapes / transitions per fit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Narrow, more layouts | 2 × 2 | 12 + 12 | 96 / 1,536 |
| Broad, more conditions | 2 × 4 | 6 + 6 | 96 / 1,536 |
| Later, more physical families | 3 × 4 | 4 + 4 | 96 / 1,536 |

Before bulk collection, verify the signed responses and known inverse on one matched pulse tape per training cell, using the first eight training tapes. Stop on a failed reset, sign or inverse check. Collect the two narrow banks once (192 tapes). Nest the broad bank within their union. Block on layout and probe seeds so each fit gets the same placement and command distribution. Keep each donor scene, trajectory and all its windows in one split. Use one 16-transition support at inference, one separate 16-command query, identical reader, optimizer, 100 × 32 draws and three paired fit seeds. Normalize evaluation errors with one frozen source-only scale. Evaluate **2 families × 2 held-out settings × 8 fresh support/query pairs** (64 additional tapes, 1,024 transitions). Use the same eight layout pairs and probe scripts across settings so the other setting supplies the matched wrong support. Score empty context on the same queries.

Advance if broad reduces equal-cell mean query MSE by ≥10% against the mean of the two narrow fits in **each** held-out setting, beats wrong and empty context in both families, and the breadth effect has the same sign across fit seeds. Otherwise stop condition expansion at this budget. Report paired layout differences and seed spread. Motion prediction alone cannot justify a native transfer claim.

**2. Does a third physical source family improve native success on another unseen family?** Retain Panda and Sawyer. Qualify two additional, distinct simulated kinematic models, for example IIWA and Jaco. Neither is qualified here. Add IIWA and evaluate on Jaco in the first fold. Reverse their roles in the second. Each fold compares the broad two-family bank with the three-family row above. Use the same frozen motor in both folds. Exclude the evaluation family from motor SFT, reader fitting, normalization and model selection in its fold. Freeze both folds before reading either outcome.

- **Gate before expansion.** Wait for R2's independent frozen source motor to reach its registered 38/50 development and 75/100 confirmation successes **per arm**. On separate qualification layouts for each candidate body, verify native reset and camera parity within body, matched scene across bodies, signed XYZ/jaw response, collision-free reach, and successful native expert trajectories. On eight qualification starts require a predeclared privileged correction using the same action interface to achieve ≥6/8 native successes and ≥2 net rescues over raw. This adds 32 native rollouts (two bodies × eight starts × two controllers). Use qualification only to stop the fixed design. A failed gate, raw ceiling or unavailable second family ends the morphology comparison. [Source gate](../libero-paligemma-dualarm-20261120-r2/PLAN.md) · [Existing simulator coverage](../libero-crossarm-20261120-r1/SIM.md)
- **Match the supervision.** Per fit, use 96 distinct successful source expert episodes, one five-action proposal/label chunk per episode, uniformly allocated across training cells (12 or 8 episodes/cell). Match placement, probe, task phase, motor, data-collection attempt cap and frozen proposal cadence. Fit 150 × 32 examples, with equal family loss weight. Use one prespecified deployment seed. Stop if balanced native labels cannot be obtained within the cap. Record rejected attempts and teacher coverage.
- **Test physical morphology at nominal interface.** Use 24 fresh support/query layout pairs on each evaluation family. Pair simulator openings, motor noise and first proposals. Compare both fits with correct support. The five controls are the three-family fit with empty or command-matched wrong-body support, the raw motor, fixed public analytic calibration and privileged calibration. This costs **48 paired cases, 336 native query rollouts**, plus qualification. Count native successes, paired gains and losses per family. Advance only with ≥3 net gains over the two-family fit on **each** family, a positive lower 95% bound for the family-balanced difference (paired bootstrap of layout pairs within family), and wins over raw, empty and wrong on both. A tie with analytic calibration leaves the need for learning open. A wide interval is inconclusive and stops escalation.

**Budget and holdout.** This audit used 0 GPUh. For future simulation, cap H1 at 256 short tapes (4,096 controls) and nine CPU reader fits. H2 reuses H1 tapes, adds at most 64 short training tapes for the two new families, plus 48 fresh evaluation supports and matched donor practices. Cap new expert collection at 384 attempts across folds. Cap H2 at 8 GPUh, excluding independent SFT. Stop if measured throughput puts the remaining work over budget. Keep UR5e exclusively for one later, separately budgeted, frozen evaluation on fresh starts. Lock the model, support rule, controller set and analysis before target access. Permit only fixed-weight inference from prespecified practice. Exclude historical target tapes, fitted target calibrations and target-based selection. The historical UR5e pilots make this a fresh-panel holdout on a previously studied family. [Prior π0.5 target panel](../libero-crossarm-20261120-r1/RESULT.md)

Two seven-joint source arms can support an arm-specific lookup or absorb differences in teacher style. Repeated layouts and optimizer seeds give no independent estimate of transfer across robot families. Even a pass on the two named development families applies to this simulated task, gripper and motor. A population scaling law, transfer across instructions and hardware performance remain open.
