## Proposed decisions, in order

1. **Practice predicts held-out responses beyond body identity.** Score frame-zero Panda/Sawyer identification from RGB and proprio separately on unseen layouts. Then cross ordinary/reduced finger friction within each body. Replay identical approach, close and push commands on paired support resets. Predict wrist translation, rotation and jaw change on a fresh layout and command script. Hold the query observation and commands fixed across genuine, independent same-body/same-setting, same-body/opposite-setting, reset-only and empty histories. Pair donors by support layout, exact commands, length and mask. Fit response scales and predictors on disjoint source layouts. **Pass** if both matched histories beat swapped and static controls on each body and the lower paired 95% bound for genuine versus swapped response-error improvement exceeds zero, clustering by layout. Otherwise stop scaling this reader.
2. **Contact history adds predictive information beyond free motion and the latest observation.** Reuse those queries. Compare commands plus RGB, full public proprio (joints, wrist and jaw), or both. Score equal-length free-space and contact windows. At a common endpoint compare the last 1, 4 and 16 transitions with the endpoint frame/state alone. Contact labels serve only for offline stratification. **Pass** if the genuine-versus-swapped advantage after contact exceeds the free-space advantage and genuine history beats endpoint-only on both bodies. Retain vision only for an incremental gain over full proprio. A policy experiment additionally needs a correction that rescues native source failures.

**Budget and limits.** This review used local evidence and 0 GPUh. A proposed source screen uses eight disjoint support/query layout pairs × two bodies × two materials × two episodes, or 64 short CPU simulator replays for evaluation. Sensor probes need separate source fitting data. Counts, horizons and pass rules are design choices. Sparse contact or wide intervals leave sufficiency unresolved. Transfer to new kinematics and gains in native reward remain open.
