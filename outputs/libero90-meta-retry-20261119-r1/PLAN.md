# Does adding a meta-trained reader improve frozen π0.5 on nominal LIBERO-90?

The headline endpoint is native success on the second attempt of a held-out task, averaged equally across task IDs. Compare the unchanged native π0.5 controller to that same frozen motor with a trained history reader selecting how many actions to execute before replanning. Both receive the budget for one identical practice on another initial state. The base ignores that practice. Each starts the retry at the identical simulator state with the same policy randomness and physical horizon. Primary inference concerns this new split of unperturbed LIBERO-90, held out from reader fitting. Record the exact native checkpoint, preprocessing, cadence, settling, version and horizon against the released client. Report any local extension to the client explicitly. This study addresses a two-attempt protocol.

## Scope and acquisition

Fix 48 fitting tasks × 20 trials, 12 development tasks × 12 trials, and 30 held-out tasks × 24 trials before collecting outcomes. The randomized task split is specific to this study. Each task uses disjoint support and query indices sampled without replacement from reset indices 1–49, excluding the earlier all-task evaluation's pose 0. Confirm actual state-bank coverage before launch. Tasks may share scene templates and motor pretraining exposure. Record per-task gains and results by scene. The task contrast asks about transfer to held-out task identities from this suite. It does not certify transfer to unseen environments or unseen backbone pretraining data.

Each of the 1,824 histories costs one default practice plus two candidate outcomes on the same fresh reset. This gives 5,472 physical simulator episodes in total, including 2,160 for evaluation. Both candidates execute the base model's proposed actions. The reader chooses one branch at retry start. Mask other-branch outcomes during single-retry training. Exclude query futures, privileged state, task identifiers and split labels from the reader. Share physical branches across ablations. Count a single selected retry, one practice, controls and policy calls for deployment cost. Also report cumulative success over both allowed attempts. Preserve all failed cases. Report one successful first attempt without practice as a separate one-shot reference.

## Inference registered before collection

Use the fully branched three-seed ensemble as the primary fitted method, with its decision rule frozen before test collection. Compare against native base first. Use two-sided 95% paired Student intervals across the 30 task means (29 degrees of freedom), equal task weights and all 24 starts per task. Report X, Y, absolute percentage-point difference, paired rescues/regressions and number of tasks improved, tied and harmed. Compute the same interval for intact versus cleared history on the same weights. Test this second comparison in fixed sequence only if the first excludes zero in the positive direction. A benchmark gain alone supports a controller improvement. A positive history attribution additionally requires the second contrast. Report the estimates and uncertainty in either outcome. Task means supply the independent working units. The three model initializations are sensitivity checks, with one ensemble prediction per retry. A generalization claim beyond this task panel also depends on scene-level sharing.

Secondary estimates include the logged versus branched difference of intact-minus-cleared gains, an independently trained current-only scorer, fixed alternate cadence, independently reassigned same-task support and the attainable native branch ceiling. Report differences with their intervals as descriptive comparisons. A same-task donor can convey valid task information. There is no persistent injected timing label on the nominal suite. Report the per-task effects and dominant scenes so that improvement driven by a single task or the alternative cadence is visible.

For planning, treat five percentage points as the effect worth detecting and use a two-sided 5% test. With 30 tasks and 24 starts each, power depends on how often success differs between the paired policies and on variation across tasks. Record sensitivity across plausible settings before launch. A confidence interval spanning zero and meaningful positive gains is inconclusive. An interval whose upper bound is below five points rules out an improvement of that size for this estimand under the working assumptions. Fix sample size, endpoint, candidates and seeds in advance. New methods after observing the test require fresh confirmation.

## Memory-enabled competitor and scope of the paper claim

Use the same frozen π0.5, same trainable reader and same support histories for an imitation competitor. On each fitting case, train it to imitate successful measured retry choices with categorical cross-entropy, distributing weight equally across successful choices and skipping query cases with no successful choice. Keep failed practices. Train the outcome scorer on success and failure labels for the same physical retries. Give each model intact and empty histories during training. Compare native success with factual practice and the within-model change upon clearing it. Report their paired difference in history gain. In the randomized single-retry setting, naïvely imitating the coin flip would train the policy to reproduce the random behavior assignment. Reward-conditioned imitation is the relevant comparison.

The cadence experiment asks whether a practice can improve a choice between two execution settings. For a general action-level claim, use a reader that conditions a frozen π0.5 action expert or outputs residual action chunks, plus a matched history-conditioned imitation model with the same output and trainable capacity. Both should execute their own closed-loop retries. Save the observations and proposals required at each query decision for that extension. Counts in the cadence design refer to discrete branches. The collection, full-action labels, live evaluation cost and power for the continuous-action design require separate accounting before launch.

Begin both arms with an ordinary goal-directed attempt by the frozen policy. Any prior outcome is admissible. Purposeful information-gathering actions would change the support policy, add another training problem and potentially trade first-attempt success for improved later performance. That requires measuring cumulative success over the entire budget. Keep active exploration separate from the fixed-practice experiment.

At 30 test tasks and 24 paired starts per task, approximate power for a true five-point gain is 91% for 10% paired disagreement and five points of between-task standard deviation. It is 73% for 20% disagreement and the same heterogeneity, and 51% for 20% disagreement and ten points of heterogeneity. These use a noncentral t approximation with 29 degrees of freedom and estimated standard error sqrt((tau² + (q − delta² − tau²)/24)/30), where q is the probability of paired disagreement, delta is the mean gain and tau is the standard deviation of the true task effects. They distinguish available precision from a guaranteed significant result and apply to the single binary paired headline contrast.
