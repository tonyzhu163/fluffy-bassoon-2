Registered before collecting or scoring target outcomes. Parent deploys the immutable code. One experiment, no policy calls, no learned reader, no GPU. Use Baige cores 38/39 only after the queryspan scorer exits and an idle-core check. Cap the physics and scoring at less than 20 minutes wall time and 20 aggregate CPU minutes. ## Prior and decision

The PR7 wider-family protocol aligned commands algebraically on a fixed physical history. Its median weak/strong singular ratio was 0.099, with large errors for unrestricted map estimates. In the subsequent command-demand check, all eight held UR5e tasks used the opening support's weak direction on layout 14, 0.63% of chunk-mean XY energy on support vs 34.24% on the query. Independent early vs late demand also increased on all eight. This justifies testing the intervention. It does not establish that probing improves predictions. The earlier fixed, rank-rich, equal-time probe worsened five-control forecasts on all three arms. The prior native compensation trial also lost successes despite closer tracking. Task-relevant excitation itself predates this work. ## Intervention, information and controls

Use the locked layout-11 nominal support's actual stored three five-control motor means, each repeated five times, on the corresponding physical arm/task. These are approximate replays because original sub-chunk actions are unavailable. Stream one chunk at a time. Finish the first five identical controls, take their mean XY unit direction, rotate it by 90° for the lateral axis. No target map, future baseline chunk, query, outcome or future replanning enters this rule. If the first vector is zero, do no probing and retain the start. Non-XY values are identical to the archived chunk means for every control and every arm. No new target-policy replans occur in this experiment.

Set requested pulse norm to the median Euclidean difference of successive five-control XY means on the 192 already archived nominal Panda/Sawyer passive supports used for the fixed source motion law. At chunk two calculate the common maximum displacement fitting both signs and both unit directions in the public unit box, capped by the source norm. At chunk three use the opposite sign, capped by the original pulse norm and that chunk's current common headroom. A smaller third pulse is retained and reported separately, including mean-translation change. Keep zero-headroom starts. Run passive and both signs of each pulse as separate 15-control attempts from the identical simulator snapshot. Average the two signs within a method before comparing. Sign averaging equates total issued XY effort of along and lateral, even when the baseline changes. Each active attempt adds the same perturbation effort as its along-line partner. Passive has lower mean effort. Report realized energy, saturation, amplitude, mean and support singular ratios. Save both commanded and actual target control directions and all seven control components.

## Physical observations and held outcomes

Use the pinned BASE arm assets, joint encoders, known grip-site FK and real software-rendered external and wrist RGB. Save time, joint position/velocity, jaw, FK and RGB on calibration boundaries; retain per-step controls, native goals and physical responses. Give each support schedule the same sensor/timing budget. The common physical reader fits measured world XY motion from commands and the source current/previous XYZ displacement law. Fit four entries of A by minimizing summed squared measured five-control XY displacement errors plus source residual_power times squared Frobenius distance to identity. Source coefficients and regularization are fixed before observing target responses. Both active and passive get exactly this estimator. RGB is preserved for readback and any later equal-information comparison. This reader uses encoders and known world geometry, so the study cannot establish a vision-only reader or an architecture advantage.

## Analysis and stopping

Average signs, then arms and draws within each task. Compare orthogonal minus passive and orthogonal minus along, reporting native, familiar, swap, general and equally weighted swap/general with 95% paired percentile intervals resampling all four task clusters, conditional on this finite panel. Report per-task and per-arm effects and achieved mean-preserving vs imbalanced cases. A gain in singular ratio or the ideal fit alone does not advance this avenue. Direction-specific reductions in both held-command and measured-motion errors against both controls on held maps would justify designing an exactly matched strong Perceiver comparison. A null, reversal or benefit confined to ideal geometry ends the present avenue. No sweep, follow-on training or native reward evaluation in this workstream. A single held tape, middle segment and interface draw per task/family limit claims across layouts, phases or embodiments.
