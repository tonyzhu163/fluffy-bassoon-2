immutable. The chosen task and20-case query bank cannot change. A fresh
initial native20-panel in the learning allocation must again score2..8;
otherwise stop before SGD without substituting another task. Initial native,
tied Gaussian and initialized bounded-SAC panels all have threshold0.

3/10 criterion scaled to20); all checkpoints remain reported. Final versus
initialized-SAC gain is separate: parameterization gain alone does not prove
learning. No favorable checkpoint selection occurs.
