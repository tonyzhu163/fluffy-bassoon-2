## Initialization, evidence and stopping proposal

Each genuinely new learner must save its **actual immutable initialized full state and RNG before its first physical reset or SGD**, including any qualification reset. Initialization uses its declared seed once; this is not reconstruction of an old actor. Preserve actor/critic/target/temperature/Adam, empty replay, sampler/global/agent/motor RNG, source cursor/config/motor/norm hashes and zero interaction/update counters.

V2 episode evidence is compact actual actor-or-unused-Gaussian identity, RNG, raw/reset/trace references and pre-SGD/post-SGD/replay clocks. Keep **one atomic rolling latest-full recovery** at normal closed recovery boundaries; do not create a distinct immutable FULL replay checkpoint for every episode. Retain immutable initial, declared milestone and final full recoveries only; final may reference its identical40k milestone. Preserve all existing recoveries; avoid redundant frozen-VLA exports. The initialized archive must precede the legacy collector's current qualification-reset ordering, so that small setup/cadence/endpoint change still needs a future exact source profile and allocated validation, not a new algorithm/framework.

Propose nominal thresholds10k/25k/40k optimizer updates **per lineage**, stopping/publishing at the first CLOSED K4 after each threshold. Each whole episode burst can overshoot by780; completing at most three remaining episodes adds2400. Thus actual milestone clocks lie in[threshold,threshold+3180]; retain actual clocks, never call them exact10k/25k/40k. No ragged K4 publication. A ceiling/deadline preserves a truthful partial prefix/full recovery rather than truncating an episode, padding a case or claiming completion.

Under this recipe, the final lineage has roughly2461–2659 total decisions and501–540 warmup-behavior decisions: an illustrative19–22% warmup share, versus the old all40 bank's65.2%. This is a counting bound, not a useful-improvement or sampled-window guarantee. The final source clocks previously ranged about5k–7k on the original bank and~29k on two later bridge leaves. More SGD exposure can still produce stationary, ceiling or regressing teachers.
