# Does a shared per-axis calibration learner transfer?

The [earlier joint reader](../libero-axis-calibration-20261119-r1/RESULT.md) often missed even familiar corrections on new development tasks. The [frozen-state probe](../libero-axis-readout-20261119-r1/RESULT.md) found partial calibration information after the support motions. Test two successive changes. Learn the signs of a persistent correction from the support and apply them algebraically to the frozen π0.5 proposal. Then constrain the same function to estimate each axis from its command and motion. Compare a joint two-output support MLP to a shared per-axis MLP, with the same raw measurements, target information, fitting examples, optimization steps and seeds. Neither learned estimator receives the command–motion product.

Reuse the prior 16 fitting reset pairs and 15 eligible development pairs. Report all results on the original task-grouped panel. Three familiar conventions train both models and the double reversal stays held out. Balance the four probe directions. Compare matched, cleared and contradictory support with identical queries, fixed final models, seeds 41–43 and 400 updates. Compare the known analytic inverse. Better double-reversal performance across seeds with maintained familiar accuracy would support this architectural choice for the simulated coordinate family. Poor transfer would favor the analytic baseline or a broader training distribution. The independent [native readout](../libero-axis-native-20261119-r1/PLAN.md) uses physical probe motions.

Baige development CPU, at most two threads, 10 minutes and zero GPU-hours. Keep evaluation-time weights fixed. The official eight transfer tasks remain sealed.
