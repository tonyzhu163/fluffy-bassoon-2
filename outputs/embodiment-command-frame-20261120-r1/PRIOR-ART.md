# What one practice tape would establish

Literature review at base `0fbc5e31`, 20 November 2026. The strongest candidate contribution is a test of **what one short, outcome-unlabelled practice episode adds to a frozen motor on a held-out arm**. Published work already covers cross-arm transfer, frozen-policy remapping, VLA conditioning on command-and-motion histories, and frozen-VLA adaptation from a robot’s own rollouts. Using PaliGemma identifies the motor under test. It supplies no priority claim by itself.

## Five primary sources that narrow the claim

| Precedent | What was demonstrated and what distinction survives |
| --- | --- |
| **Liu et al., PACE** | A controller reads a sliding window of Cartesian state, action and reward. Its training combines predictive dynamics with expert distillation. The paper tests held-out arms and varies the number of source arms from one to three to five on the same held-out iiwa. Both history-based manipulation and a source-arm breadth experiment have precedent. Our distinct comparison holds interaction cost fixed and tests a separately trained motor with practice from an earlier reset. [§§III-C–D and IV-A–B, Table I, Fig. 4b](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.19141v1) |
| **Wang et al., Cross-Embodiment Robot Manipulation Skill Transfer using Latent Space Alignment** | A fixed latent policy transfers from Panda to Sawyer and xArm6 through learned state and action mappings. The authors fit target encoders and decoders using unpaired, reward-free transitions. Their simulator collection used 10,000 trajectories of 200 steps per robot. Unlabelled robot-generated motion and a frozen task policy already coexist here. The remaining distinction is inference from one tape with the adapter weights already fixed. [§§III, IV-B and V-A, Appendix VII-D](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.01968v1) |
| **Han & Niranjani, Embodiment-Classifier Guidance** | A frozen π₀ collects its own calibration rollouts. A classifier fitted to successful chunks on both bodies then steers the motor on transfer tasks. Mean native success rose from 72% to 80% on four modified controllers, while weak-wrist results varied across arms. This directly anticipates demonstration-free frozen-VLA adaptation from self-collected data. Our narrower question removes target success labels, classifier fitting and target-based guidance selection. [§§2–4.2, Fig. 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.22576v1) |
| **Yang, Finn & Sadigh, Cross-Controller Transfer via In-Context Behavior Adaptation** | Their π₀.₅ consumes prior commanded actions and achieved motion, including context from another task. They jointly train the backbone and context encoder on demonstrations, test unseen controller modes and gains, and show that the successful wiping strategy changes with stiffness. This anticipates the history channel itself. The distinction here is a fixed motor and one prespecified probe sequence without demonstrated task behavior. A yaw-only result supports coordinate correction. Contact-strategy adaptation needs its own evidence. [§§III-C, IV-A–D and V, Figs. 2 and 4–6](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.39280v2) |
| **Open X-Embodiment Collaboration, RT-X** | The collection spans 22 robots. The RT-X experiment trains on nine manipulators and demonstrates positive transfer from pooled trajectories, including skills crossing from WidowX data to the Google Robot. The authors explicitly leave new-robot generalization open. A raw motor trained on exactly the same source data is therefore essential for attributing any added benefit to practice. [§I, Fig. 4, Table II and Discussion](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.08864) |

## Claims the experiments could support

**Current source-only screen.** A passing result would support this claim. “At 1,536 training transitions per fit on Panda and Sawyer, training on four command mappings per arm reduced mean squared wrist-motion prediction error by at least 10% against the average of two fits that each saw two mappings and more layouts. The gain held separately on +45° yaw and on +90° yaw combined with 0.65 gain.” Report each narrow bank, each arm, paired fit seeds, and layout-paired uncertainty. Correct support must beat command-matched swapped and empty support. Compare the linear estimator on the identical 16 commands and public world-frame motion. The endpoint is motion prediction on two familiar arm models. The comparison supports a choice between these training banks. [Run plan](PLAN.md) · [Registered gates and splits](../embodiment-meta-factory-20261120-r1/SCALE.md)

**Native transfer claim, conditional on a later experiment.** The sentence to earn is “On the named held-out simulated arm families performing LIBERO-Pro2.0 Goal 5 plate pushing, a source-trained adapter used one 16-command self-issued practice tape to increase paired native success of the same frozen PaliGemma motor on fresh layouts.” Report the gain, interval and number of independent starts per family. Describe scripted probes as scripted. A claim that the motor *learned to choose* informative probes requires a separate probe-selection comparison.

1. Freeze the motor, reader, normalization, probe, correction limits and selection rule before evaluation. Exclude each evaluation family from motor action training and reader fitting. Separate support and query resets. Expose only public commands and measured responses to the reader. A later UR5e panel must be described as fresh layouts on a previously studied family. [Holdout contract](../embodiment-meta-factory-20261120-r1/SCALE.md)
2. On paired starts, compare raw through an exact bypass, empty context, correct practice and command-matched wrong practice. Cross settings within arm for the command-frame claim. Swap bodies at nominal interface for the morphology claim. Match support budget, scene, noise, first motor proposal and query horizon. A current-prefix-only control is needed to credit retention across resets specifically. [Factory decision tests](../embodiment-meta-factory-20261120-r1/DECISION.md)
3. Compare a best common correction chosen on source development data, a public analytic inverse estimated from the same tape, and a privileged oracle under identical action limits. Verify native oracle headroom and opposite required corrections on the source frame control. Test distinct held-out kinematics at nominal interface for a cross-arm claim. Count raw failures rescued and raw successes broken, by family, with paired intervals. Superiority to the analytic inverse requires a paired advantage over that inverse. [Motor and information controls](../embodiment-meta-factory-20261120-r1/DECISION.md)

**Interpreting a null.** Missing the breadth gate stops expansion at this tape budget. Report whether the interval excludes the registered benefit or still includes it. If the analytic estimator reads the frame and the paired interval rules out a meaningful advantage of correct over swapped history, the measured responses carried information that the reader failed to exploit. If prediction improves but the native-success interval still spans useful gains and losses, task benefit remains unresolved. If one fixed correction matches the oracle within a practically meaningful margin, these settings provide little evidence that history is needed. If even the oracle cannot rescue the motor, the native panel cannot adjudicate the reader. Each conclusion is specific to the tested motor, task, settings and budget.
