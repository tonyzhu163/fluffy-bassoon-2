# What remains open after PACE?

**The deployment question is how to discover what a pretrained manipulation policy needs to change when it is moved to an unfamiliar arm.** A trained motor may already choose the right task, object and intended movement. The available action interface, kinematics or contact response can still turn that proposal into the wrong motion. Sometimes the original action already succeeds. The scientific question is whether a short interaction on the new body reveals which case the robot is in, and whether that information improves a later, independent attempt.

## Proposed contribution

A paper could establish **when a short, unlabelled interaction contains control-relevant information about a new body, and how much of that information a source-trained adapter can use to improve a frozen manipulation policy**. The method in this repository maps practiced command–motion pairs to a gated correction of the next motor proposal. The motor, reader, adapter and gate can all remain fixed at target evaluation. Calling this *few-shot, gradient-free adaptation from practice* makes the target interaction cost explicit. A classifier that names a robot measures recognition. Changes in the selected action and in paired task outcomes test whether the inferred property matters for control.

Three results would earn this claim.

1. **Establish that the history contains information a frame-zero cue cannot supply.** Change the hidden command map within the very same arm and scene and construct pairs that require opposite corrections. Hold out both a continuous parameter level and an unseen combination. Test matching versus mismatched practice and initial observation alone. This gives a positive control with known physics and an analytic inverse.
2. **Demonstrate physical transfer.** Under the nominal action interface, keep the checkpoint, task, opening, camera convention, probe budget, horizon and motor noise fixed. Test an excluded arm family and replicate on another family or another task. Compare raw, source-selected common correction, own practice, matched wrong practice, cleared practice, an equal-budget analytic fit, and a privileged correction through the same action interface. A matched PACE-style history-trained controller would answer the separate performance question about training a controller end-to-end. Give it equivalent source supervision or disclose extra expert/online access. Report paired wins and losses on native reward with uncertainty. The current design develops the method on simulated arms and reserves independent UR5e starts for a locked panel.
3. **Measure when adaptation pays for itself.** Compare a current-episode prefix with practice retained from a separate reset, match time spent probing for all methods, and count nominal successes broken as well as shifted failures rescued. Verify the bypass executes the original action exactly. Learned abstention is demonstrated by its nominal intervention rate and its native outcomes. To claim visual or contact-dependent inference, pass those signals to the reader and include shifts whose consequences they actually distinguish. The present reader consumes wrist motion and commands.

## Results that would change the paper

A win limited to held-out yaw or gain supports a command-calibration result. It leaves physical family transfer open. An analytic fit matching the learned reader would establish how far simple calibration goes. A valid same-interface oracle that succeeds while the learned reader fails locates the bottleneck in inferring or using context. If the oracle also fails, the panel identifies a limit of the motor or its correction interface. If practice from another body does just as well, an unconditional correction or the current query can explain the gain. The previous source reader's matching/wrong practice MSEs were 1.092/1.097 on one query layout per arm. The existing evidence motivates these experiments and leaves the proposed native transfer claim unearned. [Source evidence](../embodiment-meta-factory-20261120-r1/SCALE.md)

The first breadth study has two source families, one task and engineered command settings. It is a measurement of how to train the reader. A claim about a broadly useful new robot-adaptation method requires a stronger cross-family and multi-task result, including a manipulation setting in which an equal-budget analytic method leaves a measurable gap.
